New /design-sync scope: hover, element-selected, frame-selected, inspector-states, long-values, interactive-element, selection-lost. No-selection copies the shipped Canvas panel and adds only the requested hint. Dashed “Not in code” badges belong to the draft, never to the app.

The top bar, 240px sidebar, 280px panel, 40px header, Canvas metadata, notice line and zoom controls are copied from the shipped renderer and docs/DESIGN.md. No modes, tabs or comment editor. The example login canvas is a fixture, not a proposed product screen; its implements text is illustrative metadata.
