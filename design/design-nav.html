Design navigation — draft

Scope. Feature design-nav — docs/features/design-nav/spec.md ('완료 조건') and design.md ('사이드바 트리', '개요 페이지', '빠른 열기', '프레임 이동과 현재 프레임'). The workspace sidebar becomes a design/ tree with an Overview row; a canvas row nests its numbered frames; frame jump moves and highlights without selecting; the center of a workspace with no canvas open is the Overview of canvas cards; ⌘K Quick Open searches every saved project. Restore on relaunch has no screen of its own; its only visible part is the toast in toasts.

New. overview, overview-empty, quick-open and tree-rows are entirely new: ProjectOverview and QuickOpen do not exist yet, so those frames carry no data-doan-implements. In workspace-tree the sidebar tree and the top bar's canvas title and repo button are new; the canvas area, zoom, Inspector and Comments panel are copied from the shipped screens for context. In frame-highlight the 2 px highlight is new; the selection mark, dimensions label and Inspector are shipped. toasts reuses the shipped toast with three new messages. Copy that the feature documents do not give is labelled “draft copy” in its frame label and listed in the handoff.

Copied from shipped screens. Window 1238 × 780, top bar 40 px, the ← / repo › branch › canvas breadcrumb, the 26 px tree row with its 2 px current bar, the comment count style, the selected-element sub-row (.element-row), the canvas area and zoom, the right column (Canvas info, Inspector head and Box rows, Comments head), the focus ring, the tooltip, the toast, the Home cards and the empty-state type come from app/src/renderer/styles.css, App.tsx, Sidebar.tsx, RightPanel.tsx, CommentsPanel.tsx, Toast.tsx, Home.tsx and docs/DESIGN.md (App shell, Select and inspect, Projects home). The first-prompt button, its hint and its tooltip sentence are the shipped FirstPrompt copy (Sidebar.tsx, app/src/main/onboarding/states.ts), moved from the sidebar to the center.

/design-sync compares the main specimen and every labelled tile of all seven frames, except the shipped parts drawn for context (canvas area and zoom, mock canvas, Inspector and Comments panel, Home cards behind Quick Open), which DESIGN.md already measures. The jump highlight's fade is drawn as three still steps; the implementation measures the start step and checks timing in e2e. Native menus cannot be measured; their tile documents items and enablement only.

Draft decisions (not in the feature documents; for the conductor to accept or change): the sidebar section title becomes “Canvases” with the canvas count, and the shipped project row (name and ×) leaves the sidebar — the repo name is in the top bar and Remove stays on Home; a twisty slot precedes the kind glyph so the canvas row can expand without opening; the frame number sits in the glyph slot in --text-4 (accent when current); the open canvas row has text in --text and an accent glyph; “Selected” is a tag like Home's Open tag (accessible name “…, selected in canvas”); the Select button is the only item of the action slot; Overview sections run root first (“design”), then folders in tree order, each headed by its path; the card grid has three columns (215 px at the shipped window width); the Quick Open box is 560 px wide, 64 px from the top, with a footer key hint; the highlight holds for 0.8 s and fades out over 0.4 s.
