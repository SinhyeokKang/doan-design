### Behaviour measured in the same launch

Real-app checks of PRODUCT §5 item 2 'Prototype window' ('Play drawer', 'Links'), all passed:

- The prototype drawer opened on the playing row; End and ↑ crossed groups in visual order. A second broken link replaced the first toast.
- A link click with focus in the playing frame moved to Order confirmation and left focus in the new run's iframe, not the old run's; the drawer's playing row followed and Back returned.
- The toolbar drawer through its portal: ←/→ kept focus on a row; H and V did not switch tools or close it; Esc closed only the drawer, focus went to Play and Select stayed pressed; Enter on Play opened it on the playing row; a pick played in the prototype window with `doan active` unchanged.
- The size bubble showed Play for a selected playable frame in Select and in Hand, and none for an element.

### Documented differences from the draft

- **Description in every state.** The draft draws Play's sr-only description only while it is unavailable; the app keeps the tooltip's text as its own description in every state, since the description changes with the state (PRODUCT §5 item 2 'Prototype window' 'Play controls'), and Radix's tooltip id is left off so it is not read twice.
- **Toolbar drawer gap.** The draft places the list by hand 5 above Play; its own text and the app use `side` top with a 4 px offset.
- Rows that differ by construction or the moment of reading: the row that holds focus as a drawer opens takes the shared focus fill (the draft's static rows are unfocused; the prototype drawer's unfocused rows, Cart and Settings, match), and the app's lucide icons carry their own `class` (the draft inlines the same paths).

### Not verified

- Toolbar Play while picking, during capture and while the canvas loads (their reasons, above, are the draft's text and unit-tested; their unavailable look is the measured `tb-none` one); the draft's `drawer-groups` tiles with one group, no links and no playable frame (the grouping and the separator rule are unit-tested; the empty drawer is unchanged).
- No toast while an export's progress or result toast is up, and nothing while the run loads, failed or was lost (unit-tested only).
- A move that does not bring the window forward with another app in front, and the screen reader's announcement itself (both windows were hidden; the live region's text was read).
- DPR 1.

### Behaviour compared with the app

The app's window and the browser player gave the same answer in each step:

- Start (played from the CLI in the app, from the board's bubble in the browser): focus on the page, the live region empty.
- The name opened by pointer and by Enter: focus on the playing row; Esc: focus back on the name.
