# Design measurements

Measured values the app UI relies on, deviations from the draft canvases that are in force, and surfaces that are not verified. Draft canvases live in `.doan/canvases/`. Colour and size roles come from `app/src/renderer/tokens.css` (palette → roles → legacy aliases) and `app/src/renderer/ds/` (`controls.css`, `surfaces.css`, loaded through CSS `@import` from `styles.css`).

## Evidence and verification policy

- Checks are the static/unit/build gate plus short affected-behaviour probes under `.scratch/`, backed by `app/runtime/`. There are no screen-specific e2e scripts and no full-suite gate; new end-to-end scenarios will be designed after the app stabilizes. No value in this document implies a passing full-suite run.
- Regression pointers below name only tests that exist. A value whose only check lived in the removed `app/e2e/` suites is listed as **unpinned**.
- Raw evidence under `.scratch/` is local and ignored; the conclusions and limits are recorded here.
- **Standard `/design-sync` measurement.** A section measured against a draft canvas was checked this way unless it says otherwise: the draft canvas is read either in the app's own canvas iframe or in a second (often hidden) window of the same Electron build, and the implementation in the real app; both sides are read with `getComputedStyle` and root-relative boxes; draft and app elements are paired (by `data-doan-id`, structural path, or tag and position), and each pair compares computed properties, text and box. Every difference is either fixed or recorded in the section as a documented difference or context. Pair, row and state counts, probe scripts and their tables are raw evidence under `.scratch/` (`.scratch/design-sync/<screen>/`, `.scratch/runtime-test/<screen>/`) and are not repeated here. A section's Reference paragraph keeps only what this method does not say: the draft and frames ("Reference: <draft> frame <x>; injected: …"); which states the probe injected (IPC events, stubbed replies, forced classes or attributes, emulated media) rather than reached through the app, since that decides what stays Not verified; and the conditions that scope its px values: window or viewport size, the app build and Electron/Chromium version when stated, DPR, and any DOM or selector condition at the time of measurement.
- **Unchecked by default.** Dark mode, Reduce transparency, forced colours and native AppKit input (keys and pointer events from macOS, rather than renderer input sent by a probe) were not checked for a section unless the section names what was checked. This is a notation default, not a verification result: sections leave these four out of their Not verified lists and record only a check that was actually made (for example a window measured with Reduce transparency on, or a CDP-emulated media query) and limits specific to them. A section's statement of what a surface does under one of these conditions is its rule, not a check.
- Recurring comparison rules that apply to every screen:
  - A border style or colour difference counts as equivalent only when the corresponding border width is 0 on both sides (Tailwind preflight resolves `solid` where drafts resolve `none`); no border is painted.
  - Static draft `.focus` specimens always draw a ring. The app follows `:focus-visible`, so a pointer-opened dialog focuses Cancel without a ring until keyboard modality applies.
  - Native windows supply the outer corner radius and shadow; renderer roots have radius 0 and fill their content view. Native traffic-light placement, corner shape, window shadow and native message boxes are not measured against drafts.
  - Fixture names, paths, counts, times and error strings differ from draft sample text; their intrinsic widths and wrapping are context differences, not equality claims.
  - The native modal `<dialog>` computes `user-select: text`, `position: fixed`, `margin: auto` and a viewport-bounded `max-height`, where drafts draw static `div` specimens.
  - Inherited body type and portal placement are context, not deviations. The app body is 13px / `normal` line height with tabular figures (`tabular-nums`); a draft canvas's body is body-sm 14/20 with no numeric variant. An element that paints no text of its own (a container, a menu, popover, dialog or toast root, a text-less icon button, a `::before` dot) inherits that difference; when every text child sets its own type and matches, the difference is not recorded. Menus, popovers and tooltips that Radix portals, and dialogs and toasts the app places in the top layer or as fixed elements, are drawn in place on a draft tile or stage: their position, overflow, max width, z-index and a static stand-in's `white-space` are not compared. A section records only its own cases, such as a text part whose width the app's tabular figures change, or a placement it did compare.

## Design system

The reference for every screen.

### Overview

A light, neutral system (Light only, below): black `prominent` actions and white panels on a translucent shell, Geist type, and one accent, the chosen buddy's (Doany's violet by default), read only in the places listed under Accent places. Values live in `app/src/renderer/tokens.css`; shared components live in `app/src/renderer/ds/` (`controls.css`, `surfaces.css`) and in `styles.css`. People check the system on the design-system canvases in `.doan/ds/` (see Design-system canvases); the rules a screen follows are Adopted rules and Doan adjustments below. The system's character, mood and descriptive colour names are not settled (Open decisions).

### Adopted rules

- **Tokens first.** Every app value that a token in `app/src/renderer/tokens.css` covers (color, type size/line height/tracking/weight, spacing, radius, control and icon size, shadow, motion) is written as that token in drafts, app CSS and TSX, never as a literal. Type roles are applied whole (size, line height and tracking together): a rule that sets a role's size sets its tracking too, which `ds/checks.ts` `typeRoleTracking` checks over the renderer stylesheets (the agent notice caret, whose role size is a height, is the one exception).
  - A literal is allowed only when no token has that value; it is then either a component constant (one component or screen uses it, e.g. a wordmark size or a layout width) or a new token.
  - A new token may be added at any time when it follows the existing rules: a purpose-named family that already exists (`--space-n` = n×4px, `--size-*`, `--radius-*`, `--type-weight-*`, a complete `--type-<role>-size/line-height/letter-spacing` set, role colors rather than palette steps), a value that does not duplicate an existing token, no change to existing tokens' values, and a line in the token list below.
  - Moving a literal to a token must not change the computed value; when only an approximate token exists, the change is a design decision for the user, not a silent substitution.

- **Roles.** Code reads roles named by purpose (`surface-*`, `text-*`, `border-*`, `affordance-*`, `bg-*`), never palette steps. Text has three reading levels: `text-standard`, `text-subtle`, `text-metadata`; `text-disabled` is below the contrast targets on purpose. The primary button is the neutral prominent fill (black, white text). Status colours use the reference status roles (`text-danger`/`-warning`/`-success`, `affordance-danger`/`-warning`/`-success`, `border-danger`/`-warning`).
- **Type roles.** Size, line height, tracking and weight travel together: caption 12/16, body-sm and body 14/20, label-sm 14/20 500, label 16/24 500, body-lg 20/28, heading 24 and title 32 at line height 1.1. Regular 400 is for reading and form labels, medium 500 for actions and structure. One sans stack; the only monospace use is `--type-family-mono` (system stack, no bundled font) for code and paths in the conversation thread, and other inline code stays a sans chip.
- **Radii.** `chip` 12, `row` 16, `control` 20, `container` 24, `panel` 28px; capsules (34px) and pills are separate shapes. Below the chip, three purpose-named steps: `inline` 4 (inline code chips and glyph-sized boxes: the checkbox, the sidebar disclosure, the badge bubble), `word` 6 (word-sized highlights in a line of text: anchor chips, suggestion values, skill words) and `thumbnail` 25% (project initial thumbnails: 4 at 16, 5 at 20, 8 at 32; the first-run 28px thumbnail keeps its 7px). A spacing token is never a corner. A nested corner is `max(0, outer − inset − border)` and is not rounded to a step (`nestedRadius`: a menu row in a 24px menu with a 4px inset and a 1px border is 19px).
- **Focus.** An outer 2px `border-focus` ring at a 2px offset on buttons, icon buttons, checkboxes and chips; the ring does not change geometry, and every button variant uses the same ring. Rows inside a scrolling list (tree rows, menu rows including the worktree rows, Settings project rows) and preference radio rows (Settings' buddy rows, the storage choices in Project Settings and the Set up dialog, the question card's options) use an inset ring at −2px so the list does not clip it. Text fields show focus with their own border (`border-prominent`), not a ring. The conversation composer card (Home's and the docked one) is the exception: a 1px `border-field-focus` ring with `--shadow-sm`, under 3:1 on its own, so the caret and the shadow carry focus with it.
- **States.** Pressed changes the fill only, never the position. Selection stays visible apart from hover. Disabled is a role (`text-disabled` on `affordance-disabled`), never `opacity`. The shared button hover and pressed fills skip `:disabled`, `[data-disabled]` and `[aria-busy="true"]` alike (`ds/controls.css`), so a `data-disabled` button (a refused Cancel, a toast's running action) keeps the disabled fill and a busy one its resting fill under the pointer and while pressed with no screen rule; an `aria-disabled` button without `data-disabled` still draws its look on its screen. An unavailable control shows the default cursor, never `not-allowed` or `progress`.
- **Unavailable but focusable.** A control that its own press can make unavailable stays focusable: `aria-disabled` with a press that does nothing, never native `disabled`, which drops focus to the page. This covers a control pressed to a limit (Zoom out / Zoom in, Back / Forward), a busy action, a trigger whose list is being read, an unavailable menu row (still in the arrow order, reading its reason) and a choice held while its form's request runs (the Doan files radios during Set up or a clone). Native `disabled` stays for a control that is off as a whole (the zoom controller with no canvas drawn, Send with an empty field).
- **Busy action.** While its request is out, the pressed confirm or recovery button keeps its fill and label, shows the spinner before the label, and is `aria-busy` and `aria-disabled`; a Cancel that is refused meanwhile takes the disabled look (`aria-disabled`, `data-disabled`), and a text field turns read-only. A Retry inside a notice or a page keeps "Retry"; only a toast's running action changes its label (Retrying) on the disabled fill. A Retry beside a failure line (a Settings save or read, the buddy save, the thread read error, the Home and docked composer notices, the design presets read in the presets popover and the `/` list, the canvas page's “Couldn’t load the canvas”) keeps that line and itself on screen while its request is out (`retryButton` with `useRetry` in `ListRetryButton.tsx`, the thread's `retrying` read, or the canvas pane's `canvasRetry.ts` state through the reload's loading status); only a success clears the line, and focus that was on the Retry then moves to a target on the same surface, such as the first remaining control, the canvas region, or the Overview's Conversations heading when the recovered list is empty. A confirmation whose request can fail (conversation Delete, Move to Trash, MCP Remove, the buddy switch, MCP Add) stays open and busy until main answers, refusing its Cancel and Escape; Move to Trash and MCP Remove show a failure above the actions and associate it with the confirm button so the person can retry. MCP Add restores the editable URL and associates the inline error with the field. A failed buddy switch closes the dialog, returns focus to the pressed buddy and shows Retry in General. A failed conversation Delete closes the dialog, restores focus and shows a toast unless the app is quitting. Every Retry of one conversation list (the thread-place notices, the Overview's right panel and the three title menus) runs while any of them reads it; a menu row keeps its highlight and focus while it runs. When that Retry fails again the line's text changes by a no-break space, so its live region reads the same sentence again; a list read made for another reason that fails again is not announced again. A spinner inside a button is one shared rule: 14 px, in the button's own ink, its ring at 30 % (`ds/controls.css`); a spinner beside a sentence keeps the 16 px DS spinner.
- **First-load lines.** A first-load line (a spinner and a present-participle label) shows only 300 ms into its load, so a quick load never flashes it: the Overview and panel threads, the problem overlay's reading line, the sidebar's Reading row, the Overview's Conversations list, the prototype window and the canvas page's Loading line. The Overview and panel threads, Canvas conversations list and prototype window mount an empty status region before the text appears. The project reading overlay mounts its status with the text. The sidebar describes the tree item and hides its spinner from the accessibility tree. A failure shows at once.
- **Label ellipsis.** A progress label is a present participle without an ellipsis (Checking, Connecting, Reading usage, Sending, Stopping, Updating, "Waiting for Notion", "Still reading 2 projects"); a command that opens further input keeps the macOS ellipsis (Add Projects…, Settings…, Export…). A command that goes to a Settings section takes none (Open Agents settings, Manage agents, Sign in again, Update, Sign in to an agent). Exception: the canvas toolbar's Export icon button is named and tooltipped “Export canvas” without one; the canvas row menu keeps Export….
- **Button case.** In-app buttons and menu items use sentence case (Keep draft, Set up project, Setting up, Stop and remove, Add file or folder, Create repository); product, screen and section names keep their capitals (Remove from Doan, Edit on Home, Open Agents settings). Exception: native menus, native panel titles and macOS-style commands keep title case (Show in Finder, Check for Updates, Restart to Update, Move to Trash).
- **Accessible names.** A control's accessible name starts with its visible label, so voice control can say what it sees (Retry → "Retry checking Codex", "Retry downloading Doan 0.2.0"); a name that would only repeat the label is left to the label. A Retry beside a failure line is named by its word alone and described (`aria-describedby`) by the line that says what it retries.
- **Motion.** `time-fast` 120ms, `time-state` 150ms, `time-panel-enter` 180ms, `time-settle` 220ms; `ease-enter`, `ease-settle`, `ease-state`. A dialog rises 12px over 220ms as it opens and drops 4px over 120ms as it closes (`@starting-style`, `transition-behavior: allow-discrete` on `display` and `overlay`). A tooltip that waited for its delay settles in (fade, 2px rise, scale 0.97, 2px blur); one opened instantly from a neighbour does not animate. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the fades stay and every movement, scale and blur goes.
- **Floating material** (menus, popovers): 24px corner (16px compact), 4px list inset (2px compact), a transparent 1px border that still counts in the nested-corner arithmetic, `shadow-panel` drawing the visible edge (`CanvasText` border under forced colours). Glass (`surface-elevated-glass`, 90% white with an 8px blur) applies only under `prefers-reduced-transparency: no-preference`, outside forced colours, where `backdrop-filter` is supported; otherwise the surface is opaque. Menu rows are 32px with the derived 19px corner (13px compact). Menus are capped at 320px, except the conversation menu (`.conv-menu`: Project Overview, the canvas conversation panel and the prototype window), which is 360px wide. A listbox row that is not a Radix item takes the menu row's `--affordance-floating-hover` while focused. Once focus is on a row of the list the pointer moves it onto the row under it (the preset popover's cards, as the frame list's rows do, without the keyboard ring); with focus elsewhere, such as the popover's search box, the row fills on hover alone and focus stays put. The `/` list keeps its rows unfocusable and moves its one highlight with the pointer instead. Over the sidebar column a web `backdrop-filter` has no effect (measured in Electron 45: glyphs under a blurred panel there stay crisp, and blur fully once the column has an opaque background); the column is transparent over the window's native vibrancy. The sidebar's project and canvas row menus (`.shell-side-menu`) therefore take the opaque `--surface-popover` and no blur; menus that open over the opaque panel keep the glass.
- **Dialogs.** Four widths: compact 400px, standard 640px, wide 880px, expanded 1152px. Compact is centred; the others hang 128px from the top and may grow to 16px above the window edge. 32px padding (16px in a window 480px wide or less), 28px corner, opaque `surface-popover`, 16px between parts. The header is the title over an optional description; actions sit at the end of the row, Cancel first and the completing action last, at the default button size, in every dialog, confirmations included. There is no Close button; Cancel and Escape close every Doan dialog. The modal scrim is `bg-scrim` (black at `#00000060`).
- **Tooltip.** A near-black capsule (`affordance-prominent`, `text-on-prominent`): caption type, 4/8px padding, corner half its one-line height (12px; 24px tall), no arrow, at most 384px wide with long paths wrapping. A pointer opens the first tooltip after 700ms and, within 300ms of the last one closing, the next at once (Radix `delayDuration` / `skipDelayDuration`; the export viewer's toolbar tooltip waits the same); keyboard focus opens it at once. The capsule takes no pointer events and is not hoverable: the pointer leaving the trigger closes it even toward the capsule, and a click on it reaches the control underneath, also while it fades out.
- **Tooltip use.** Every icon-only button has the shared tooltip naming its action; a sidebar row's disclosure chevron, part of the tree row, does not. An `aria-disabled` control shows its reason in the tooltip instead of its label, and keeps the same text as its own `aria-describedby` description. The shared tooltip replaces every native `title` in the renderer (iframe titles aside, which are accessible names): a name cut by an ellipsis shows there in full only while it is cut (`NameTip`, measured on the box that ellipsizes, when the tooltip would open), and information only the tooltip carries (the canvas path, an anchor chip's detail, Send's unavailable reason, a preset's description, a frame row's name, the Eye and Play marks, the playing chip's note) shows on every hover. Inside a modal dialog the capsule is portalled into the dialog. A shortcut is a key chip after the label (`.doan-tooltip.has-key`), never text in the label. A tooltip over a row of a menu or popover list hands an Esc on to its host (`TooltipEscape`), so one Esc closes the menu.
- **Toast.** Floating material, 384px wide, 12px inset; the message and its one action share a row, the action a `prominent` sm button on the right that stays vertically centred while only the message wraps. It sits at the window's bottom right, `--space-4` (16px) from both edges, on Home and over a workspace or canvas. It takes `--shadow-floating`, and enters and leaves as the GitHub star card does (fade and 8px rise over `--time-settle` with `--ease-enter`; fade and 4px drop over `--time-fast` with `--ease-state`, the element kept drawn and inert as `data-closing` until then); a toast that replaces another keeps the element and does not move again.
- **Shared variants.** Screens add variants instead of overriding shared sizes or fills.
  - `doan-button` `quiet` (drawn as the sm suggestion chip; no app markup uses it): `--affordance-backdrop-hover` fill with a `--blur-md` backdrop blur (`--affordance-subtle` without blur under reduced transparency); hover adds one inset `--affordance-backdrop-hover` layer, pressed or open one `--affordance-backdrop-pressed` layer. A disabled `quiet` button is transparent like a ghost.
  - `doan-button` `trigger` (a value that opens a menu): 8px inline padding, no fill at rest, `--affordance-backdrop-hover` on hover and while open, `--affordance-backdrop-pressed` on press.
  - `doan-button` `lg`: min-height `--size-control-lg` 48, padding `--space-3` `--space-8`, label 16/24; only Welcome uses it.
  - `doan-button` `xs`: min-height `--size-control-xs` 24, padding `--space-1` `--space-2`, caption type, 14px icons; the docked composer's agent, effort and permission pickers (measured in Project Overview), the queue's Send now and the thread's background-work Stop.
  - `doan-icon-button` size `xs`: 24px (`--size-control-xs`), pill corner, 14px icon; its ink comes from the screen (a chip × is `--text-metadata`).
  - `doan-icon-button` `accent` (Home Send): pill on `--affordance-accent` with `--text-on-accent`, hover and pressed on the accent hover/pressed roles; disabled takes the shared disabled fill, and `accent` is excluded from the ghost disabled transparent rule.
  - `.doan-dialog` with `data-ui-presentation="search"`: Quick Open (see Design navigation).
  - `doan-button` `panel` (`data-ui-variant="panel"`): the secondary button inside an inset notice, where `subtle` would lose its fill (both are `--neutral-2`). Rest `--surface-panel`, hover `--affordance-panel-hover`, pressed `--affordance-subtle-pressed`, disabled the shared filled disabled rule. On the inset fill the fill measures 1.09 (rest), 1.12 (hover) and 1.28 (pressed) to 1, the label 19.17, 15.64 and 13.71 to 1. Used by secondary actions inside inset notices, for example the conversation strip's Deny, Always allow, Skip and Edit on Home, the thread's and the docked composer's notice actions and the Overview's moved-line Open.
  - `.doan-notice.doan-panel` (notice on a non-white backdrop, `ds/surfaces.css`): see Notices.
  - `.doan-code` (`ds/controls.css`): the inline code chip in the sans face: body-sm type, `--surface-inset` fill, `--radius-inline` corner, `0 --space-1` padding, one line cut with an ellipsis on its text span, and an optional leading swatch (`i`) at `--size-icon-xs`. Only the canvas inspector's token rows use it so far; the six hand-written chips in `styles.css` are not moved to it yet.
  - `.doan-menu[data-ui-variant="tree"]` (`ds/surfaces.css`): a menu of nested rows (`role="tree"` / `treeitem`). Each row's `--tree-depth` moves it in by one icon plus `--space-2`, so a child's 16px `--text-metadata` icon starts under its parent's name; one 1px `--border-standard` guide (`.guide`, `--tree-level`) per ancestor runs down that ancestor's icon centre to its last descendant row; the selected row is pressed (`--affordance-backdrop-pressed`); a row fills while focused, or hovered while the tree has no focus, so one fill shows at a time. The canvas inspector's element menu.
- **Scrollbars.** Native and thin (`scrollbar-width: thin`, `scrollbar-thumb-quiet` on a transparent track) through `.doan-scroll`, the one class for it, which every modal dialog takes; Radix ScrollArea is forbidden.
- **Style loading.** The main window's `index.html` links `fonts.css`, `styles.css` and `doany.css`; `styles.css` brings in `tokens.css` and `ds/index.css` (and through it `controls.css` and `surfaces.css`) with CSS `@import`, because importing CSS from JS is blocked by the dev CSP. Cascade order under the strict CSP in production and DEV, with no entry CSP warning, was last observed with an earlier stylesheet set and is not re-measured for this one.

### Doan adjustments

- **Accent ramp.** Each buddy has its own ramp, and the chosen buddy's is the accent (`tokens.css`; drawn with the contrast table in `.doan/canvases/buddy-accent.html`). None is a reference family.
  - **Violet (Doany, the default).** Each step takes the per-step OKLCH lightness and chroma share (relative to step 9) of the reference's twelve-step light ramp whose step 9 carries white text, at hue 305, gamut-clamped by reducing chroma; the step values, contrast ink (white), pressed and link are in `tokens.css`. White on step 9 is 5.21:1 (APCA Lc −80), and hover and pressed are darker than step 9, so white only gains contrast.
  - **Friends** (`--puri-*`, `--dungdung-*`, `--popo-*`, `--dori-*`). Steps 1–8, 11 and 12 keep violet's OKLCH lightness and chroma (gamut-clamped, chroma at most 1.2 × the friend's step 9) at the hue of step 9; Dungdung's steps 1–6 take 2.2 × violet's chroma and 7–8 1.6 ×, so they read yellow, not cream. Step 9 is the buddy's body colour moved by Doany's own body-to-violet-9 shift; Puri's hue moves to 168 and Popo's to 42, away from the success green and the danger red, and Popo's 9 is darkened until white clears 3:1 on it. 10 and pressed keep violet's offsets from 9. The step values are in `tokens.css`.
  - **Icon colour** (`-icon`, read as `--text-accent`): step 9 darkened in OKLCH lightness until it reaches 3:1 on a pressed sidebar row (`--affordance-backdrop-pressed` over `--bg-app`, `#d9d9da`), the darkest surface an accent icon or dot sits on (values in `tokens.css`); Doany uses violet 9, already 3.70:1 there. Measured on that row: Doany 3.70, Puri 3.02, Dungdung 3.05, Popo 3.00, Dori 3.07 to 1.

### Notices

Reference: `.doan/ds/notice.html` (frames `tones`, `surfaces`, `content`), implemented in `app/src/renderer/ds/surfaces.css`.

- **One style.** Every notice is `.doan-notice`: inset `--surface-inset` fill, 12px 16px padding (`--space-3` `--space-4`), 6px gap (`--space-1h`), `--radius-row`, 14/20 body-sm text in `--text-standard`, and a 16px icon on the first text line: the `doan-alert-icon` (CircleAlert, `aria-hidden`), or in that slot a spinner, the provider mark (the conversation strip and the thread's permission and question notices), or a notice's own glyph (Info, CircleStop, the moved line's ArrowUpRight). Every notice has the icon, whatever its tone. Notices differ only by tone and surface.
- **Tone.** `warning`, `error` and `ok` colour the whole text (`--text-warning`, `--text-danger`, `--text-success`) and the icon follows it (`currentColor`); the parts of a notice's text take no ink of their own, except code chips, links and buttons, which keep theirs, and the reason line: a notice's second line giving main's own account of a failure (Project Settings, the Project step) is a caption (12/16 with its tracking) in `--text-metadata` in every tone. At 14/20 the text measures, on the inset fill and on the panel fill: neutral 17.59 / 19.17, warning 10.44 / 11.37, error 11.41 / 12.44, ok 11.31 / 12.32 to 1.
- **Surface.** On a plain opaque white backdrop (Settings and the other home-panel pages, the conversation strip's band, dialogs, side panels and white cards) the notice keeps the inset fill. On any other backdrop (first run's transparent overlay, Home's gradient, the preset popover's glass over Home) it adds `doan-panel`, which changes only the fill (`--surface-panel`) and adds the panel shadow.
- **Alignment.** The icon and the text lead on the first text line; an inline action or an action group centres vertically in the notice, so beside a two-line text it sits between the lines. An sm action reaches into the padding with −6px block margins, so a one-line notice is 44px with or without an action. The rule reaches every notice with an inline action: the conversation strip, Project Settings' notice (its actions move to the middle when a reason caption adds a line), the composer and buddy Retry notices (when their text wraps) and a one-line notice, where it changes nothing. A notice with two or more actions can stack them instead (`.doan-notice.stacked`): the action group takes its own row at the notice's end, under the text, 8px below it (`row-gap: var(--space-2)`), right-aligned, without the −6px margins, so the 12px bottom padding stays; the Project step, the sign-in and MCP notices (first run's Agent step, Settings › Agents and MCP Connectors) and the Notifications hint stack every notice with two or more actions. A thread notice also puts its actions on their own row under the text.
- **Actions and margins.** A notice holds its actions: none sits under or beside it outside its fill (first run's Agent step, Settings › Agents, MCP Connectors and Notifications included). A secondary button inside an inset notice uses the `panel` variant; on a `doan-panel` notice it stays `subtle`, where `panel` would lose its fill. Screens set only outer margins on a notice.
- **Toasts.** The window has one toast at a time: a new toast replaces the one shown, its action included. A refusal of what the person just added to a composer (an attachment over the limit or one main refused) is that composer's own notice, on Home and in the docked composers, not a toast; the canvas capture's failure stays a toast.
- **Regression pointers.** `ds/controls.test.ts` (shared metrics, tones, `doan-panel` as fill only, no ink on text parts, the reason line's caption and metadata ink, margins only from screens, action alignment, the stacked row, the `panel` variant).

### Colors, layout and depth

What Adopted rules does not already say, read from `tokens.css` and `styles.css`.

- **Three colour layers** (`tokens.css`): the palette holds the only literal colours (neutral ramp, the five buddy ramps — violet for Doany and `--puri-*`, `--dungdung-*`, `--popo-*`, `--dori-*` — red/amber/green status hues, and the eight Tailwind 600 project identity colours, kept apart from the status hues); roles name a purpose; legacy names (`--bg`, `--accent`, `--text-2` and the like) alias roles so drafts drawn before this system resolve. No renderer code reads the legacy layer.
  - **Buddy switch.** Between palette and roles, `--buddy-*` holds the accent steps the roles read (Added tokens). `:root` points it at violet; the renderer sets `<html data-buddy="<id>">` for a friend (none for Doany), and that `:root[data-buddy]` block re-points only `--buddy-*`, never a role or a legacy `--accent*` name. The accent roles (Accent places) read `--buddy-*`; the sidebar roles stay violet. Drafts link `tokens.css` without `data-buddy`, so they draw violet. `@theme inline` reaches no buddy ramp and no `--buddy-*` name.
- **Project identity.** A project thumbnail (`.shell-thumbnail`, `styles.css`) is the project's initial on one of the eight `--identity-project-*` roles chosen by `projectHue`: the fill is the colour 14% into `--surface-panel`, the letter the colour 65% into `--text-standard`. 16px in the sidebar, Quick Open and the Overview header, 20px in Home's project picker, 28px on first-run cards, 32px in the Project Settings header.
- **Spacing.** `--space-n` is n × 4px, with `--space-half` (2px) and `--space-1h` (6px); named spacings (`--space-row-gap`, `--space-control-inset`, `--space-section-gap`, `--space-chip-inset`) alias steps.
- **Shell layout** (`styles.css`): a 56px header (`--size-chrome`), a resizable sidebar (`--doan-sidebar-width`, 220px by default, see 'Sidebar'), 8px gutters, and the screen's body on white with no edge shadow: Home's (and Add Projects', Settings' and Project Settings') `.home-panel` with `--radius-container`; the workspace's main area and its right column (280px by default on the Overview and resizable; the canvas page's resizable conversation panel, see 'Canvas area and right panel') joined into one body with 24px outer corners. The shell is transparent over the native sidebar material, `--bg-app` under reduced transparency.
- **Depth.** Four shadows: `--shadow-panel` (floating material and the raised surfaces on a panel, such as the composer tray, canvas notices and the prototype's Fit frame box; its hairline ring draws their edge; body panels have none), `--shadow-floating` (toasts and the GitHub star card; the same ring with a wider drop), `--shadow-sm` (dialogs, tooltips), `--shadow-xs` (the switch thumb). Glass is `--surface-elevated-glass` with `--blur-sm` on floating material and `--blur-md` on the `quiet` button. Surfaces without a shadow are told apart by fill: shell `--bg-app`, panel `--surface-panel`, inset `--surface-inset`.

Judgment calls nobody has confirmed, each shown on the canvas named.
