# Ledgerly

Ledgerly is a web app that freelancers use to send invoices and get paid by card.

- Primary users: independent designers and developers who invoice a handful of clients each month, usually on a laptop between client work.
- Plans: Free (up to 3 clients) and Pro ($12 per month, unlimited clients). There is no other plan and no annual discount.
- Payments are processed by card. Ledgerly never stores full card numbers.
- Voice: plain, calm and specific. No exclamation marks in product copy.
- `styles/tokens.css` is the single source of truth for colors, type and spacing; `components/` holds the shipped component CSS.
