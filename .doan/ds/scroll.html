scroll

.doan-scroll: a thin native scrollbar (--scrollbar-thumb-quiet on a transparent track); .doan-menu scrolls the same way. Scroll containers keep native scrolling; Radix ScrollArea is not used. The box, its overflow and its rows are canvas content. With overlay scrollbars the thumb shows only while scrolling.
