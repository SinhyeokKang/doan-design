floating

.doan-floating: the material under menus and toasts. The base is the opaque popover fill; glass (--surface-elevated-glass with --blur-sm) applies only without a reduced-transparency request, outside forced colours, where backdrop-filter works. Its 1px border is transparent so it still counts in the nested-corner arithmetic; --shadow-panel draws the edge. Both materials sit over stripes here; the opaque one is restated as .is-opaque.
