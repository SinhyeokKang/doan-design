TooltipContent (components/ui/tooltip.tsx) adds .doan-tooltip: a near-black capsule in caption type with no arrow, 4px from its trigger. A long path wraps inside the 384px cap. It settles in with a short rise and fade after its delay, and appears at once when another tooltip was just showing.

With a key

A tool's tooltip names its key after the label, on one line, 8px apart (.doan-tooltip.has-key). The key is the shared doan-kbd variant for a dark material, data-ui-variant="on-prominent": a fill of the tooltip's ink at 16% and its ink for the letter, no ring. Its sm size (caption type, 16 high) keeps the tooltip 24 high; the default size (28 high) is the canvas picking hint's Esc.
