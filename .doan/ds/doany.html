doany

Doany: the mascot as a button ("Make Doany hop") in a square slot: 114px on Home, 142.5px on Welcome. The still image, padded 20%, shows until its textures load; then a WebGL canvas fills the slot and animates the hop. The canvas draws only the still: .doan/assets/doany/transparent/doany.png is byte-identical to app/src/renderer/public/doany/doany.png, which is outside the served scope. The focus ring is restated as .is-focus.
