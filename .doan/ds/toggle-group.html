toggle-group

.doan-toggle-group: chrome icon buttons wrapped in one track, so a group of choices reads as a single control and needs no divider from its neighbours. The track is --affordance-backdrop-hover with a --space-half inset and a --radius-chip corner (the 10px item corner plus the 2px inset); two 32px items make it 68 × 36. One item is pressed (aria-pressed); it rises onto --surface-panel with --shadow-panel, as the current tab does in mode-tabs, and keeps that fill on hover and mouse down. Unpressed items use --text-subtle and the chrome icon button's hover and pressed fills, so they step over the track. Markup: role="group" with a name, items with data-ui-placement="chrome" and their own names.

States

Default · Fit pressed, Responsive not
Hover on the unpressed item · --affordance-backdrop-hover over the track
Hover on the pressed item · stays raised
Mouse down on the unpressed item · --affordance-backdrop-pressed
Mouse down on the pressed item · stays raised
Keyboard focus · the shared ring, outside the item
Responsive pressed
No item pressed
Disabled · --text-disabled, no fill; the track stays
