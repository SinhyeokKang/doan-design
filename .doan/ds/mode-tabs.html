mode-tabs

The .shell-modes capsule: a translucent --affordance-backdrop-hover track holding 32px tabs, the current one raised on the panel fill with --shadow-panel. The window header centres it as page tabs; Home's preset popover and Quick Open reuse it as filter tabs (.group-tabs), static and tighter, with 14px icons. All of its rules live in styles.css and are restated here, hover as .is-hover.

Open: the capsule writes its corners and sizes as literals (24px, 20px, 16px, 40px, 32px) that equal --radius-container, --radius-control, --size-icon, --size-control and --size-control-sm instead of reading them. Whether to move them onto the tokens is undecided.
