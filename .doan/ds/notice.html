notice

.doan-notice: an inset fill, never a coloured edge, with body-sm text and the alert icon (or a spinner) on its first line. The tone colours the whole text and the icon follows it. On a backdrop that is not plain white, the notice adds .doan-panel, which changes only its fill and adds the panel shadow. An sm action centres on the first line and reaches into the padding, so a one-line notice keeps its height.
