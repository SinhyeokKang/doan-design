icon-button

.doan-icon-button: a 32px square (--size-control-sm) with the --radius-icon-button corner and a 16px icon. It shares the button's fills per emphasis. Hover, pressed and focus are restated as on the button canvas; the chrome placement is drawn on the shell fill it sits on.

Defined in ds/controls.css but used by no markup: the md size (40px) and the prominent and panel fills on an icon button. They are not drawn.
