.shell-thumbnail: the project's first letter, upper-cased, on one of eight identity colours picked by hashing the name (projectHue, projectInitial in shellNavigation.ts). The fill is the colour 14% into --surface-panel, the letter the colour 65% into --text-standard. Its rules live in styles.css and are restated here.
