shadow

Four shadows. Floating material and the raised surfaces on a panel (the composer tray, canvas notices, .doan-panel cards) take --shadow-panel, whose hairline ring draws their edge; body panels have none. Toasts and the GitHub star card take --shadow-floating, the same ring with a wider drop; dialogs and tooltips take --shadow-sm; the switch thumb takes --shadow-xs. Surfaces without a shadow are told apart by fill: shell --bg-app, panel --surface-panel, inset --surface-inset.
