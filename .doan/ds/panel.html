panel

.doan-panel: a rounded, opaque card raised on a backdrop, such as first run's cards and lists and notices on a backdrop that is not plain white (--radius-container, --surface-panel, --shadow-panel, whose hairline ring draws its edge). A screen's body panel is not a .doan-panel and has no shadow (see shell-layout). It draws no content of its own. The text and padding below are canvas content.
