button

.doan-button: a capsule (--radius-capsule) in label-sm type. Variants are data-ui-variant, sizes data-ui-size; states come from native and Radix selectors. Hover, pressed (:active) and focus are restated on this canvas so they sit side by side; "pressed" is drawn with data-state="open", which the same rule covers. Panel is drawn on the inset fill it exists for, quiet on a decorated background.

A trigger that opens a menu keeps its hover fill while open; only a press deepens it. An open disclosure (aria-expanded without aria-haspopup) is not drawn pressed.
