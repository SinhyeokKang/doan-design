answer-popover · draft · 10 frames

Answer an agent’s request from a popover above the composer.

Scope: docs/features/answer-popover/ (spec ‘확정한 결정’ and ‘완료 조건’, design ‘자리, 쌓임, 크기’, ‘끝 여백과 따라가기’, ‘포커스, 키보드, 알림’ and ‘드래프트 요청’). Waiting permission requests and questions leave the thread and show in one popover pinned to the bottom of the thread area of the canvas panel, the Overview and the prototype window, one request and one question at a time. Every frame is new (Not in code); the existing draft agent-questions.html is left as it is.

Frames /design-sync compares: question-one, question-steps, permission, queue, collapsed, stack, states, widths. motion plays the motion on a loop and draws start and end states; copy is the wording table. Chosen in the app: surface A (floating white), collapsed bar A (the head alone), the cap min(70%, 100% − 40 px) at every size, Back and Next wrapping to a second row only when narrow, motion at 400 ms. The candidates not chosen were removed. Focus rings and tooltips are drawn statically.

Copied from shipped screens

  • The question card’s content and states (Settings group and rows, ob-radio, doan-checkbox, Other input, Send · Skip · Stop, spinner, inline error) and the permission notice’s content order (summary, reason, saving detail, option buttons, Stop): ConversationThread.tsx QuestionCard and the permission case, styles.css, docs/design/conversation.md ‘Agent question card’.
  • The folded lines (activity-head ask-answered), message bubble, agent text and the thread gaps of each host; Jump to latest; the docked capsule composer, its tray, queue rows, schedule line, reason line and the / list: ConversationThread.tsx, DockedComposer.tsx, dockedRules.ts, limitReset.ts, styles.css. Shared buttons, inputs, tooltip, floating surface and menu items from app/src/renderer/ds/.

References

  • None outside Doan. Placement and stacking come from the feature documents; motion uses Doan’s own motion tokens.
