Select and copy canvas text by dragging

In Select, a drag that starts on canvas text (4 px on screen, no modifier keys) highlights the range; ⌘C or Edit > Copy copies it as plain text. A still click still selects the element. The press never reaches the canvas: the canvas script holds a Range and the app paints the line rects of its Text nodes on the overlay, over the glyphs, in the person's accent at 30 % alpha with a 1 px panel ring. Feature documents: docs/features/canvas-text-copy/.

  • All three frames are new. The selection and hover marks (.mark, .mark.hover) are restated from app/src/renderer/styles.css as shipped; the mock canvases use .doan/assets/mock.css. Only one range is visible at a time, so each tile shows one.
  • /design-sync compares the highlight (.tr) and the marks in surfaces and drag: fill colour and alpha, ring, one rect per line box, and stacking (highlight under the selection and hover marks).
  • There is no new UI copy, toast or menu. Success gives no feedback (macOS ⌘C convention).
  • References: the jump highlight's color-mix(in srgb, var(--affordance-accent) 45%, transparent) glow and the selection mark's 1 px --surface-panel ring, checked in app/src/renderer/styles.css; the I-beam on a panel halo follows .doan/canvases/canvas-hand-tool.html; macOS text selection drawn behind text in the accent, recalled from memory (here the overlay lies over the text because the canvas is another document).

Press, drag, release and the frame boundaryNew

The frame stays selected throughout (solid mark). The hover mark hides while dragging and comes back on release, even with the pointer still. During the drag the cursor is the text cursor everywhere on the canvas; V, H and Space are ignored and Esc ends the drag and clears it.
