canvas-drawing-cards · draft for review · 4 frames

A turn shows each new canvas it draws as a card at its end.

Scope: docs/features/canvas-drawing-cards/ (spec ‘완료 조건’ 1–11, design ‘renderer: 카드 항목’). A new file. /design-sync compares only the canvas card (.canvas-card with its icon, name, status and Open) and the stack’s gap, in every frame: running, asking, ended, many. New, not in code: the card and its stack. Everything around them is copied as shipped and only places the cards.

  • The card replaces the shipped handoff and missed lines in the original thread (.marker.handoff, drawn in canvas-handoff.html, which stays as it was). The receiving conversation’s handoff card (“Handed off from …”) is unchanged; the canvas card borrows its surface: inset fill, row radius, no edge stripe.
  • Copied as shipped: the canvas conversation panel head and thread, the Overview’s thread column (720), the activity row, the turn indicator (TurnIndicator, drawn still: its sweep is not part of this draft), the Send now message’s Sending line, the permission notice, the Stopped notice (ConversationThread.tsx, styles.css, read in this session). Tokens and primitives are linked from app/src/renderer/.
  • Example project acme-web. The person asked for a checkout and an account canvas; the turn made checkout-flow.html and account-settings.html. The provider is Claude Code.
  • Icons are lucide file, forward, ban, circle-stop, chevron-down, square-pen, panel-right-close (lucide-react 1.61.0; file read from app/node_modules in this session, the others copied from canvas-handoff.html). The spinner is the shipped .doan-spinner at icon-sm.
  • Presets read: harden (every state, long names, many cards). References: none outside Doan.

03

After the turn Card new, not in code

Each end state alone. The status never repeats the file name. Drawn: the turn ended (done, failed, stopped or cut off by a restart) with no handoff. Handed off: Open goes to the new conversation on that canvas. Refusals, cancel and missed keep the shipped reasons with the ban icon; a refusal for the conversation’s own canvas and a canvas gone since have no Open, and gone adds “No longer in <project>” to whatever the status was. The last tile: a stopped turn, where the spinner stopped at once and the card stays after the shipped Stopped notice.
