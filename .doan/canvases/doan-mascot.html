Meet Doany.

Doany (pronounced “Doh-nee”) is Doan’s plush companion on Home. Breathing, independent eyes, soft body deformation and a spring-driven beret. Original WebGL motion code, MIT licensed; no paid runtime or external requests. This is a design study, not an implemented product change.

Reference: existing buzz-home canvas and docs/DESIGN.md Home measurements; selected six-lobed mascot restored to its original pre-accent lavender palette. Body and hat layers generated with GPT Image. Preset read: animate. Welcome reuses Home’s Doany above the shipped wordmark and introduction, with an ~80 px character and Get started in the same height-centered group, offset upward 72 px. Home remains ~64 px and offset upward 64 px. Position from available height, never a fixed top margin.

Static storyboard · Not a running V2 animation

Illustrative whole-image poses only. They do not measure peak travel or simulate the beret spring. The runnable prototype must use separate layers and match the selected still at rest.

Rest~3.31 s breath cycle. Initial blink at 2 s; then every 2.5–6 s, 260 ms each. Preserve a smooth Pebble outline.
AttentionPointer target held 1.8 s; typing looks toward the composer for 1.4 s. Keep the existing smoothed gaze.
HopClick, Enter, Space or an accepted Home send. 180 ms anticipation, 570 ms flight, 600 ms settling. Repeated input restarts the hop.
BeretKeep the spring response; measure a new pivot for this right-tilted hat. No headroom clipping.
Reduced motionEyes open, still pose. No hop, breathing or gaze tracking. Inputs remain usable.
Hidden / failedStop time advancement and repeated drawing while hidden. Use the V2 still for WebGL or texture failure and context loss.

Implementation boundary

Reuse the existing time model. Replace V1 textures, eye coordinates and hat anchors; remove six-lobed radial deformation. Preserve slots, focus, send guards and WebGL disposal. No new engine or dependency.

Browser preview checks passed for loading, hop, typing, keyboard, reduced motion and context-loss fallback. A sampled hop sweep retained headroom. Final visual approval, sustained performance and real-app checks remain pending. Review the runnable V2 Home and Welcome drafts above before app implementation. Source: doanyMotion.ts, Doany.tsx, doanyRig.ts, docs/DESIGN.md. Preset: animate.

Motion study · Try Doany

Enlarged for review. Every action is interruptible; the Home layout stays still.

Soft body. Curious eyes.

One focal response: a small, eager hop after Send. Between actions, breathing and occasional glances keep the character present.

At rest3.3 s breath cycle. Random blink every 2.5–6 s; each blink lasts 260 ms. Slow, independent glances.
Pointer / typingEyes lead; body turns gently. Typing draws the gaze down to the composer. Exponential smoothing, rate 5/s.
Hop180 ms anticipation → 570 ms flight → 600 ms settling. The beret follows with a damped spring.
Reduced motionStatic resting pose; no bounce, breathing or tracking. Input stays usable. Off-screen and hidden-tab rendering stops.

Home character: about 64 px inside a 114 × 114 px drawing area. The drawing area occupies normal layout space above the title. The whole group is centered in the content panel with 32 px minimum padding and a 64 px upward optical offset; short panels scroll. Extra drawing space keeps the beret inside the canvas throughout a hop. WebGL unavailable: original transparent PNG. Generated layers are a prototype, not final animation artwork.
