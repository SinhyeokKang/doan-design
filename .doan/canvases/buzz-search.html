Quick Open, as a search palette.

Scope: the Quick Open dialog’s surface, field, result rows and empty states (buzz-restyle child 4, docs/features/buzz-search/). Every frame is new and is compared by /design-sync: the dialog, its field, list, rows, matched letters and status line. Behaviour is unchanged (search scope, matching, order, the 50-result cap, keys, focus return), except that the key hint line and the esc chip are gone and a click outside the dialog closes it like Escape (2026-10-30 user). Results are grouped by project (one group per root, headed by the project thumbnail, project and branch, 2026-10-30 user); filter tabs (All, Canvases, Frames) narrow the list by kind (2026-10-30 user). The surface is a proposed presentation of the shared dialog (data-ui-presentation="search"), approved 2026-10-30.

Copied from shipped screens: the header and the LNB (TopBar in app/src/renderer/App.tsx, app/src/renderer/ShellSidebar.tsx), with their CSS taken from .doan/canvases/buzz-home.html, which copies app/src/renderer/styles.css; the shared dialog, scrim and controls from app/src/renderer/ds/ linked in place; the empty-state copy from app/src/renderer/QuickOpenDialog.tsx. Canvas-only parts: the page and captions, the wallpaper behind the glass (approximates native sidebar vibrancy), schematic traffic lights, the simplified Home and workspace bodies under the scrim, the dialog drawn open in place instead of in the top layer, and the state tiles that show the dialog without its window. Project, canvas and frame names are illustrative and follow the QuickOpenDialog.test.tsx fixture.

References: Buzz block/buzz-app at c40dfdf4, read with git show on 2026-10-30 (local HEAD ec1b9acc): src/app/shell/PageSearch.tsx (search presentation, outside-click dismissal), src/app/shell/SearchChoices.tsx and SearchChoices.css (field, results scroll and fade, 44px rows, 24px icon slot with 21px icons, two-line labels, empty line), src/shared/design-system/styles/overlays.css (dialog 40rem at 8rem, border and shadow kept by the search presentation, 12px inset, panel radius plus 4px, panel fill), components.css (navigation-item search variant), materials.css (the results list uses the panel interaction recipe: row hover is affordance-subtle), src/features/search/MatchedLabel (1px underline at 2px).
