Suggested prompts that wrap to two lines

Scope: item 1 of docs/features/small-ui-tweaks only. The suggested prompt rows (.suggestion, .suggestion-label) shown in the new-conversation state of the canvas conversation panel (280 and 320 wide), the project Overview (960 × 600 and 1238 × 780) and Home. The question for review: once one-line and two-line rows are stacked, can you still tell where one button ends and the next begins?

Each frame shows, left to right: Today (shipped, one line with an ellipsis); A · two lines (the proposed wrap, no other change); B · two lines, gap 8 (the gap between rows goes from 4 to 8, --space-2); C · two lines, divider (a 1 px --border-standard line centred in the unchanged 4 px gap, inset 12 at both ends like the shared menu separator .doan-menu-sep). C is the chosen option (decision 5 in design.md, confirmed by the person 2026-11-06): the divider stays visible next to a hovered, pressed or focused row, with no rule hiding it. A and B stay on the board as the options it was compared with. Hyphenated canvas names may break after the hyphen (decision 6).

Item 4, on C only (new, not in code; the person, 2026-11-06: “하이라이트 치고 싶음. 연보라 배경에 보라색 medium”; confirmed in the app the same day, design decisions 7–8): the dynamic value, the project name in “Document the design system of …” and the canvas name in “Review / Polish the … canvas”, takes the anchor chip’s look (.anchor-chip, styles.css 580) as an inline highlight that wraps with the sentence: --surface-accent-subtle fill, --text-accent-subtle ink, --type-weight-medium, 6 px corners, 0 / 6 (--space-1h) padding, each line’s piece cloned. Today, A and B are drawn without it. The chip component itself is not reused. Frame 05 keeps the compared options: the slice break (decision 8) and the real anchor chip, which cannot wrap and ellipsizes past 180 px (decision 7).

Copied from shipped code, not redesigned: the row, icon tile, arrow, gap and hover / pressed / focus values (app/src/renderer/styles.css 734-748 and 1014, DESIGN.md ‘Home’ ‘Suggestions’); the containers’ insets (.cp-scroll 8 / 16 / 0, .ov-col 720 max with 24 at the sides). Row widths follow from that CSS: panel 280 → 247, panel 320 → 287, Overview 388 at 960 × 600 and 666 at 1238 × 780, Home 680. Panel heads and composers are placeholders for context only.

New, not in code: the two-line label rule (-webkit-line-clamp: 2, overflow-wrap: anywhere, row align-items: center kept, decision 3) and the chosen divider C (option B is drawn for comparison only). Sentences are real suggestedPrompts labels (homeComposer.ts 333-355) for canvases in this repository, plus two made-up long names to show the clamp and an unbroken name. Hover, pressed and focus are drawn with classes standing in for :hover, :active and :focus-visible.
