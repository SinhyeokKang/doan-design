Suggested prompt button

Variants for a button only the suggested prompts use, replacing the shared doan-button (quiet, sm). Variants A, B and D show the group in the middle of the empty area; the chosen C sits right above the composer on the Overview and in the canvas conversation panel, and 32px under the Home composer. The second button in each surface shows the hover state. Not in code: variants A–D and the centered placement; sentences are the real suggestedPrompts labels for a project with canvases.

The shared quiet button, start-aligned right above the composer. For reference.

A · Outline pill

No fill, a hairline ring and full pill radius. The lightest of the four; it reads as an option, not a control. Wraps into centered lines.

B · Tile card

Icon on top, sentence below, three equal cards with a hairline ring; a sentence wraps to two lines instead of truncating. In the narrow panel the cards become one column with the icon at the start.

A faint violet fill with violet ink and no ring. Says "Doan suggests this" most clearly, but adds violet to the empty state: the accent places list in DESIGN.md would change.
