thread-motion · draft · 4 frames · entry A, reveal B

The thread moves smoothly: a sent message enters, a reply unfolds, the view follows.

Scope: docs/features/thread-motion/ (spec ‘확정한 결정’ and ‘완료 조건’, design ‘진입 모션’, ‘출력 점진 표시’, ‘스크롤 따라가기’ and ‘드래프트 요청’). All motion is new, not in code. Every tile replays on its own (a click on the board selects instead of pressing); in Play prototype the buttons work too. Chosen (2026-11-25): entry A, the rise (16 px over 400 ms, time-thread-enter); reveal B, words that fade in over 400 ms, at a 400 ms lag cap. Compared and dropped: a flight from the composer, settling in place, paced words without a fade, and whole blocks. stream keeps today’s chunked jumps beside B for comparison; it is not compared by /design-sync. /design-sync will compare the entry animation by computed style; the reveal and the follow are compared by their constants (in this file’s style comment and script) and a recording.

Fixed by the feature documents: every message this window sends enters (direct, Send now, a queue going out, a new conversation’s first); the reveal never lets the oldest hidden character get older than 400 ms, finishes the tail in 150 ms on message.done, and shows everything at once on Stop, failure, turn end, an item after the reply, a selection or a window coming back; while the reply grows the view stays pinned to the bottom every frame, and only a jump (an entering message, a new item) is followed with an exponential ease (τ 40 ms, about 220 ms); a person’s wheel, scrollbar drag or scroll key stops following. Under reduced motion the entry only fades, the reply shows as it arrives and the view jumps.

Scroll · how the view follows New, not in code

The board cannot wheel, so the person’s scroll is simulated. Following stops at a person’s wheel, scrollbar drag or scroll key, and waits about 100 ms after the last wheel event (trackpad momentum) before it may resume once the person is back at the bottom. A jump to a card stops following until the person returns.
