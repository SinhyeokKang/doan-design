thread-motion · draft · 3 frames · choose entry and reveal

The thread moves smoothly: a sent message enters, a reply unfolds, the view follows.

Scope: docs/features/thread-motion/ (spec ‘확정한 결정’ and ‘완료 조건’, design ‘진입 모션’, ‘출력 점진 표시’, ‘스크롤 따라가기’ and ‘드래프트 요청’). All motion is new, not in code. Press each tile’s button to replay it. Choose one entry (A, B or C) in entry and one reveal (A, B or C) in stream; the rest is removed once chosen. /design-sync will compare the entry animation by computed style; the reveal and the follow are compared by their constants (in this file’s style comment and script) and a recording.

Fixed by the feature documents: every message this window sends enters (direct, Send now, a queue going out, a new conversation’s first); the reveal never lets the oldest hidden character get older than 250 ms, finishes the tail in 150 ms on message.done, and shows everything at once on Stop, failure, turn end, an item after the reply, a selection or a window coming back; while the reply grows the view stays pinned to the bottom every frame, and only a jump (an entering message, a new item) is followed with an exponential ease (τ 40 ms, about 220 ms); a person’s wheel, scrollbar drag or scroll key stops following. Under reduced motion the entry only fades, the reply shows as it arrives and the view jumps.
