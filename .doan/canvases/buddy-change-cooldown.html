buddy-change-cooldown — a confirmed buddy switch and its 24-hour lock in Settings

A redesign of Settings > General for the buddy-change-cooldown feature (docs/features/buddy-change-cooldown/). Picking another buddy there now asks first, and a confirmed switch locks the choice for 24 hours. First run’s Buddy step and the buddy screen are unchanged and never start the lock. Every frame is new and none is in code. /design-sync will compare general-confirm, general-confirm-saving, general-locked, general-locked-minutes and general-failed.

Implementation spec

  1. Rows. The shipped Buddy rows. Clicking another buddy’s row, or Space or Enter on it, opens the confirmation; the radio does not turn on (prevent the native check). Arrow keys only move focus between rows, wrapping. The saved buddy’s row does nothing.
  2. Confirmation (frames 01–02): the shipped compact SettingsDialog alert, title “Switch to <Name>?”, description “You can’t change your buddy again for 24 hours.”, Cancel (subtle, focused first) and “Switch to <Name>” (prominent). While it is open the Settings page lock applies: Search, the header modes, Back and the other categories show unavailable. After Switch it stays open until the save answers, with the shared spinner in the verb and both buttons aria-disabled; Escape does nothing.
  3. Lock (frames 03–04): the problem overlay’s material (--surface-panel at 60 % with --blur-sm; opaque --surface-panel without blur under reduced transparency) over the group only, following its corners; the section title, description and notices stay uncovered. Inside, centred: the chosen buddy as the Home Buddy (Doany.tsx, animated in the app; drawn here as its still and shadow) in a 96 px slot, 16 px above the problem overlay scene’s type (.ob-heading.problem-heading): the heading is the remaining time as a live HH:mm:ss countdown in the heading role (24 / 26.4, 500, tabular figures), ticking every second with seconds rounded up (24:00:00 right after the switch, 00:00:01 in the last second), and 12 px below it the subtle body-sm line “Until you can change your buddy again. Your buddy stays for 24 hours after each change.” The heading and the line describe the hop button (in that order) and have no live role. The hop button’s ring is the shared focus ring and shows only while it has keyboard focus; frame 03 draws it because focus moves there after the switch. The rows underneath are inert.
  4. Failure (frame 05): the shipped notice and Retry above the group, unchanged; no lock until a save succeeds.
  5. Not drawn: a newer settings file (rows off as today, no overlay) and an unreadable one (no lock): both are the shipped buddy-choice.html frames.

Copied from shipped code: the Settings header, sidebar, page and Buddy rows are buddy-choice.html’s settings-general frame (checked against SettingsGeneral.tsx, BuddyPicker.tsx and DESIGN.md ‘Settings · General’). The alert is SettingsDialog.tsx with ds/surfaces.css; the scrim and page-lock marks follow mcp-connectors.html; the overlay material is .workspace > .problem-overlay in styles.css; the buddy slot is doany.css. Copy follows Doan’s clarify preset. Focus rings in frames 01, 02, 03 and 05 show where focus is: Cancel when the confirmation opens, the pressed verb while it switches, the buddy after the switch, the pressed row after a failure.
