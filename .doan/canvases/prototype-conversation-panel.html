The prototype conversation as its own panel

The draft of feature prototype-conversation-panel, which redesigns the conversation panel shipped from prototype-conversation.html (that draft stays as it is). The conversation is an independent panel on the window’s right that reaches the window’s top, so the header ends where the panel begins. While it is open the header has no conversation button: Hide conversation is in the panel’s head, which shows only the conversation’s title, since the conversation cannot be switched in this window. Everything else (thread, composer, playing frame chip, buddy, states, toasts, Fit behaviour) is shipped and is not redrawn. Frames: open-1024, open-640, open-from-360, name-rows, panel-titles, focus-close, compare; all are new. Until the feature’s branch merges, this draft is the /design-sync source for the panel’s layout and head; prototype-conversation.html is not.

  • Copied: this page’s restated styles.css rules, sprite, header, mock frame, thread, composer and suggestions come unchanged from prototype-conversation.html (which restates styles.css at 4ed73b3f, and the header from prototype-responsive.html and prototype-cli-export.html). The panel head is the shipped ConversationPanel head (.doan-conv-head, .doan-panel-hide with PanelRightClose and the tooltip “Hide conversation”) holding the shell’s own static title h2.phead.rp-head (ConversationPanel.tsx, styles.css .right.ov-right .rp-head). Panel limits and the start-edge handle are the shipped canvas panel’s (DESIGN.md ‘Panel width’, ‘Resize handle’).
  • New, not in code: the data-panel="open" layout (replacing the shipped attached one) (the panel’s place, corners, shadow and gap), the header without the conversation button while open (R 104), the name rule measured on the area left of the panel, the head without title menu and New conversation, the title’s ellipsis rule.
  • References: all read in this repository (the drafts and files named above, DESIGN.md ‘Frame prototype’ and ‘Canvas area and right panel’, PRODUCT §5 item 2 ‘Prototype window’, docs/features/prototype-conversation/); no outside product was used.

01

Closed and open at 1024 New, not in code

A 1024 window keeps its size when the conversation opens. The panel is its own surface on the window’s right: x696, 8 from the window’s top, end and bottom, 320 wide (the canvas panel’s default max(320, 28 %)), all four corners 24 and --shadow-panel, with no 1 px start border. It runs up through the header row, so the header covers only the 696 to its left (the header element 680, in the shell’s 8 px inset). The frame panel keeps x8, y56, and ends 8 before the conversation panel: 680 wide. The header’s conversation button leaves the header while the panel is open, so the right group is the toggle and Export (R 104) and ends at L − 16 = x680 (the toggle starts at x576). The name fills x200 to 568 (368). Hide conversation is the panel head’s end button. The start-edge handle resizes the panel from 280 to min(50 %, window − 360) = 512, the shipped limits. All tiles in Responsive.

02

Closed and open at 640 New, not in code

A 640 window is the minimum while the panel is open, so it keeps its size; the panel opens at 280 (default 320 cut to the maximum min(320, 280)). The area left of the panel is 352 (panel x352), below 408 = R 104 + 304, so the name takes the header’s second row: x16 to 336 at y56, the header 100, the frame panel from y100, 336 wide. Closed at 640 the name was in the first row (R 140: 276 wide); opening moves it down 44 and the frame panel with it, with no animation. The panel itself does not move with the header rows.

03

Opening from a 360 window New, not in code

Below 640 the window first widens to 640 inside the work area (the shipped rule) and the panel opens at 280. At 360 the name was already in the second row (R 140 needs 444), and in the opened 640 window it stays in the second row (left area 352 < 408), so the header stays 100 and the frame panel stays at y100; the frame panel goes from 344 to 336. Closing keeps the window at 640 and gives the panel’s 280 and its 8 px gap back to the frame panel (624).

04

Name rows while open New, not in code

While the panel is open the name’s row is decided by the area left of the panel (the panel’s x = window − panel − 8), not the window: the first row while it is 408 or more, where the name is 80 (x200 to x280), the second row below that. The renderer can read it from the header element’s own width (left area − 16, so 392). With the panel at 280 the edge is the 696 window; at the default 320 it is 736. Dragging the handle crosses the same edge: the same capsule changes rows and keeps focus and an open menu, as the shipped rule does.

05

Panel head titles New, not in code

The head is the shipped conversation panel head (.doan-conv-head from ConversationPanel.tsx) with the panel shell’s own static title (h2.phead.rp-head) in its title slot and Hide conversation at its end; there is no title menu, chevron or New conversation, because this window cannot switch conversations. The title is the canvas’s current conversation’s, read as the main panel reads it (canvasConversationTitle): the list’s summary title (“Checkout Review details button” in the other frames), or before the list names the conversation its first message line with chips by name (“Checkout The Review details button does nothing when I click it.” here). One line with an ellipsis; the full title is its native title only while it is cut, as the frame name’s tooltip is. With no current conversation the title is “New conversation”, the main panel’s words for that state, over the shipped suggested prompts. While the list loads, after a list read failure, or when the canvas is gone or its id is duplicated, the title is “Conversation”, the panel shell’s own title, since none of these is a new conversation. Text starts 16 in from the panel, as the main panel’s title button text does. The head keeps the shipped 56 (--size-chrome), as the canvas page’s and the Overview’s conversation panel heads do; in a panel 8 below the window’s top the title centres at y36 and Hide conversation sits at y20–52, 8 below the header’s controls (y12–44).

06

Focus when opening and closing New, not in code

Opening (the header’s Show conversation, by pointer or key) moves focus into the composer, as shipped. When the composer cannot take focus (before the window has its first view the panel body is empty, with no composer), opening moves focus to the head title (h2.phead.rp-head, tabIndex −1, the main window’s focusPanel target) with the shipped ring 2 px inside its edge; once the composer can take focus, focus moves there once if it is still on the title. While the panel is open Tab goes header (Back, Forward, Reload prototype, the name, the mode toggle, Export) → panel (the handle, Hide conversation, thread, composer) → frame content: the panel comes before the frame in the DOM, so the frame’s own Tab stops or focus trap cannot keep focus from it. Closed, Tab goes header → frame content. Hide conversation shows the shared 2 px ring under keyboard focus and its tooltip “Hide conversation”, 4 under it. Closing returns focus to the header’s Show conversation, which is back at the right group’s end. The header button is one element kept in the DOM and hidden while the panel is open, so focus has a stable place to return to (POSTMORTEM 2026-11-13).
