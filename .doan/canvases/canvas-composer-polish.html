03

Tools row · unavailable Picker and Export New, not in code

Unavailable buttons are aria-disabled, never disabled: they stay in the focus order and in ←/→, and their tooltip gives the reason. They take the shipped unavailable tool look (not-allowed cursor, no hover or pressed fill). Picker is unavailable exactly when the composer's Pick from canvas is, with the same reason. Export is unavailable when the row menu's Export… is (a row read error, that canvas exporting), on a page that is not a listed canvas, and on a project problem. While the page is locked it stays usable-looking and does nothing, like the row menu button. While the save panel is open and while exporting, focus stays on Export; when the save panel closes it returns there. Progress shows only in the existing toast.

Canvas conversations · row Delete New, not in code

The title menu's row Delete, on the Overview's right panel rows: a 24 px doan-icon-button with the 16 px Trash2 in --text-subtle, a sibling of the row button in its li, over the time's spot (the time's visible text is clipped, its spoken label stays; the spot keeps 24 px), centred on the first line, its glyph flush with the time's end. It shows while the pointer is over the row or the button, and while keyboard focus is on either; otherwise it is clipped to 1 px. Tooltip “Delete conversation”; its name is the title menu's, Delete “<title>”. Working or waiting: aria-disabled, --text-disabled, no fill, default cursor, reason as tooltip and aria-describedby; a press does nothing.

Tab and Shift-Tab move between a row and its Delete; ↑/↓, Home and End move between rows only. Pressing it opens the same confirmation as the title menu; after a delete focus goes to the next row, else the previous, else the panel title; Cancel or Esc returns it to this Delete.
