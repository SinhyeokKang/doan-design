Canvas inspector — a column between the board and the conversation

A read-only Inspector gets its own column on the canvas page, left of the conversation. It opens, folds and resizes as the conversation panel does, so the thread keeps the column's full height. The inspector's contents are not drawn: it is empty while something is selected, and shows its empty state while nothing is.

How to try it

  • Play the frame (marked 1470 × 920, a 13" MacBook Air's default size) or Option-click on the board. On the board, the controls above the frame set the window width and read the board's width; they are not app UI.
  • Select: the first click in the Payment frame selects the frame, the next one the element under the pointer; Esc steps back; a click on empty board clears. Hand (H) or holding Space drags the board; − / + zoom.
  • Hide inspector / Show inspector, Hide conversation / Show conversation and Hide sidebar (⌘B) fold and unfold with the shipped 180 ms motion. Drag a column's edge to resize it; a double-click on the edge restores the default.

Proposed, to settle in the feature

  • The inspector opens and closes only by the person, and its shown or hidden state and width are remembered on this device for every canvas, as the conversation panel's are.
  • While hidden, Show inspector sits at the end of the canvas header after the divider, before Show conversation; Hide inspector sits in the inspector's own head.
  • Icons: with two right panels, the header's Show buttons name what they open instead of a panel shape: Show conversation changes from lucide panel-right-open (shipped) to message-square, Show inspector takes sliders-horizontal. Each panel's Hide keeps panel-right-close, since it sits in the panel it closes.
  • Width: 280 by default and at least 280; at most what leaves the board 360 beside the conversation's width, the rule the conversation panel already follows. Empty state copy: “Select a frame or element on the canvas to see its properties.”

Copied from shipped screens
