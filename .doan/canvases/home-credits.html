Home credit line — exploratory draft

A quiet copyright line at the bottom of Home’s body, followed by the repository address and email. The user asked to see a canvas before any feature documents exist, so this is a proposal, not a decided draft: /design-sync compares nothing yet. Every frame is new. Only the credit line (marked New, not in code) is proposed; everything else is Home as shipped.

  • Purpose: give Home a quiet footer below the greeting, composer and suggested prompts.
  • Shared footer treatment: © 2026 Doan, repository address, and email, separated by vertical bars; 12px type retained, lighter ink, a 40px minimum gap after the suggested prompts (after the composer when there are none), and a 20px bottom inset. All frames use this treatment; the vertical separators have 40% opacity.
  • Contrast, accepted: --text-disabled on the white panel is about 3.0:1, below WCAG AA’s 4.5:1 for 12px text. The user keeps it on purpose, so the line is barely visible.
  • Year: “© 2026” shows the build year. At implementation the year comes from the build, not from a fixed 2026 in the copy.
  • Link and email: only the repository address is a link; it opens https://github.com/SinhyeokKang/doan-design in the browser. The email is plain text the person can select and copy (.selectable, as the app does for text on its user-select: none body), not a mailto: link, because the app opens only https links (app/src/main/openLink.ts). Copyright text is plain text. The link shows its destination directly, with no tooltip.
  • Copied from shipped code: the window, header, sidebar and Home panel markup and styles.css come from .doan/canvases/github-star-prompt.html (its restatement of styles.css at 4d983d90, whose Home, shell, toast and star card rules are unchanged on develop f7fc6e6a; checked with git diff). Changed against that source to match the code: the Attach and agent buttons’ names (DESIGN ‘Home › Roles and names’), the toast and star card rules now in styles.css, and the stage’s top inset, which HomeStage (HomeComposerView.tsx) writes inline from stageInsets: for this markup’s group (545.2 high with three one-line suggestions) that is the 32 minimum at both window sizes. DESIGN ‘Home › Stage position’ records a 473.2 group and a 57.4 inset, measured when the suggestions sat on two rows; that difference is not re-measured in the real app here. The star card is StarPrompt.tsx as shipped. Focus ring and type use the shared primitives (the 2px --border-focus ring and caption type).
  • Proposed change to PRODUCT §5 item 1 ‘Home’: “The body holds only the chosen buddy (below), a greeting, the composer, a Home-only gradient and up to three suggested prompts” would gain the credit line. The GitHub star card (PRODUCT ‘App (Mac)’) is unchanged; the credit does not depend on its record. Out of scope 5 needs no change: the repository page is already one of the links Doan opens in the browser, and the email opens nothing.
  • References: the Claude.ai Home screenshot the user provided (checked: three small, light grey, centred lines at the very bottom of the page, items separated by vertical bars, some of them underlined links). The idea (quiet, centred, at the bottom, underlined links) and the vertical bar separators follow it; type, ink and spacing are Doan’s tokens.
