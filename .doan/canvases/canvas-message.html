canvas-message · draft · 4 frames

Hand off to an existing canvas, and send a message to its conversation.

Scope: docs/features/canvas-message/ (spec ‘보이는 것’, design ‘영향 받는 부분 · 앱 renderer’ and ‘드래프트 요청’). States and copy on existing parts only, no new styles. Drawn in the canvas panel at 347 unless a tile says otherwise. All four frames are new and not in code; /design-sync compares all four.

  • source-cards: the sending turn’s canvas cards for send (waits, Sent to, Queued in, the target conversation deleted), a handoff to an existing canvas, and the chain refusals. handoff has no “earlier in this chain” refusal: it always starts a new conversation.
  • received: a message that arrives mid-conversation through send, in the received bubble, with the sending agent’s provider logo and the group name “Message from <name>”; its turn failing or stopped shows no Edit and resend.
  • queue: an agent message queued in the receiving conversation (the sending agent’s provider logo, × only; the source is named only in the row’s accessible name), mixed with the person’s rows, and the row left after a restart.
  • toasts: what shows instead of moving the screen, for every send and for a handoff from a turn at step 1 or later.

Built on the thread-polish draft, not on code

  • The received bubble, the meta line inside it and the cards’ subtle Open are the confirmed draft .doan/canvases/thread-polish.html (frames agent-bubble, card-open), restated here; thread-polish is not on develop yet. Same rules: the person’s bubble fill and width, the meta at the top over a divider, “From” for screen readers only, the name cut to 3 em, the time hidden in threads 275 px or narrower, Open as the panel xs button. For via: send the meta’s first icon is the sending agent’s provider logo (the shipped providerLogo images, here .doan/assets/provider-logos/) instead of forward, and the group name is “Message from <name>”.

Copied from shipped screens

  • Panel, thread, Markdown, activity line, end notices (endView in ConversationThread.tsx), docked capsule composer and queue rows (DockedComposer.tsx, styles.css ‘.queue’), toast (Toast.tsx, ds/surfaces.css ‘.doan-toast’), the shipped handoff toast copy (handoffToastText in handoffView.ts) and the card copy (canvasCardText.ts).

Proposals to confirm

  • Toasts: “<source> sent a message to <target>”, “<project> Overview sent a message to <target>”, “<source> handed off or sent to <n> canvases”, “<source> sent messages to <n> canvases”.
  • Card Open for Sent and Queued: the accessible name “Open “<conversation title>” on <file>”.
  • The received bubble’s group description: “Sent from “<source conversation title>” · <time>”.

References

  • The send icon (source cards only): lucide send, checked in app/node_modules/lucide-react (v1.61.0).
  • No outside tool was referred to.
