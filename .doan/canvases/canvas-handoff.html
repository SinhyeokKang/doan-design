canvas-handoff · draft for review · 6 frames

A canvas conversation hands its work off to the new canvas it made.

Scope: docs/features/canvas-handoff/ (spec ‘완료 조건’ 3–7, design §6, tasks D1). A new file. /design-sync compares every frame below: source-requested, source-handed-off, source-refused, source-cancelled, handoff-card, toasts. New, not in code: the four hand-off lines in the original conversation, the handoff card in the receiving conversation, and the two toasts. Each frame’s heading says which parts are new.

  • Confirmed by the person (2026-11-05): canvases are named by file name, as the shipped “Moved to <file>” line does; the reason, cancel and toast wording and the ban icon; the card’s look; refusals carry no Open; when a canvas later disappears, Open is hidden (not disabled) and the text ends with “· No longer in <project>”, on both the handed-off line and the card head.
  • Copied as shipped (d7137e85): the canvas conversation panel head and thread (ConversationPanel.tsx, CanvasConversationPanel.tsx, ConversationThread.tsx), the promotion line .marker (“Moved to …” + Open) that the hand-off lines extend, the permission notice, the Stopped and failed notices (endView), the activity row, the toast (Toast.tsx, ds/surfaces.css). Rules restated from styles.css; tokens and primitives linked from app/src/renderer/. Each tile is cut under the thread; the docked composer is unchanged and not drawn.
  • Example project acme-web. The original conversation is on drafts.html; the turn made checkout-flow.html (frames payment, shipping). The provider is Claude Code; other providers differ only in the logo and the name.
  • Not drawn as its own frame: the screen move itself (design 결정 5). After a move the target canvas shows with the panel open on the receiving conversation and focus on the card — the first tile of handoff-card. A queued message moved to the receiving conversation uses the shipped queue row.
  • References: none outside Doan. Wording chosen with Doan’s clarify preset after the shipped promotion line, endView and openLinkRefusal (quickOpen.ts), read in this session. Icons are lucide forward, ban, circle-stop, chevron-down, square-pen, panel-right-close (lucide-react 1.61.0, read from app/node_modules in this session).

Toasts · hand-off off screen Two new messages, not in code

When the receiving conversation starts and the person is not on drafts.html (or a page lock, dialog or first run is up, or they are typing in the original composer, or the list read takes over 10 s), nothing moves and one shipped toast shows at the window’s lower right with Open. Open opens checkout-flow.html with its panel open on the receiving conversation. If the canvas disappears while the toast is up, Open answers with the second toast, after the shipped deep-link refusal.
