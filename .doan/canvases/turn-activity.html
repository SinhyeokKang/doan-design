turn-activity · draft for review · 11 frames

While the agent’s turn runs, the thread says what it is doing.

Scope: docs/features/turn-activity/ (spec ‘완료 조건’ 1–7, design §1, §4 and ‘초안 요청’, tasks D1). A new file. Every frame is new, not in code: the turn indicator does not exist yet. Six forms are drawn over the same states (1)–(12) so the person can pick one (design decision 1), and the pinned line is drawn as the second position (decision 10). After the pick, /design-draft turn-activity --revise keeps the chosen form, position and copy, and /design-sync compares those frames (with their tiles in widths and reduced-motion).

Open — needs the person’s choice

  1. Form (decision 1): A status line, B reply placeholder, C inset card or D divider rule. E and F reopen spec conditions 1 and 4 and decision 4 and are drawn only to compare. Proposed: A.
  2. Position (decision 10): the thread’s last item (frames 01–06) or the line pinned above the composer (position-pinned). Proposed: pinned.
  3. Jump to latest with the pinned line (pinned-jump): S1 stacked, or S2 folded into the line. Proposed: S1.
  4. Copy and elapsed format (copy-format): the stage words and “0s · 59s · 1m 0s · 1h 4m 12s”. Proposed as drawn.

Fixed by the feature documents (drawn the same in every form)

  • One line, fixed height; a long path ends in an ellipsis and the time keeps its place. No token count, no second line, no progress or remaining time, no “Still working…”.
  • Shows from the render where the person’s message enters the thread until the turn’s end; gone while a permission card or question waits (7); the activity rows keep their spinner and running head, so two progress marks can show together (5), (3).
  • The time counts from turn.started in whole seconds and never restarts. Under reduced motion the moving part stops and the words still change.
