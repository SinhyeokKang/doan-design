The person chose on 2026-11-06 (design decisions 1 and 10): the form is the reply placeholder, the position is inside the thread as its last item (the reply’s place), and the stage copy and elapsed format are the ones in copy-format. The forms and the pinned position compared in the first draft are removed. This revision awaits the person’s final confirmation.

Sheen (round 4, 2026-11-06, at the person’s request; rounds 2 and 3 tried accent colours, now dropped): the stage words are dark neutral, --text-metadata (6.4:1 on the panel) as in the first draft, and a strong, narrow white streak sweeps across them: --text-metadata mixed 80 % in oklch with --surface-panel, the band 30 % of the words’ width and its brightest core 10 %. At its brightest the letters dip to 1.4:1; the person accepted this as transient, since the band passes in a moment and the rest of the words stay at 6.4:1. Under reduced motion the words stay --text-metadata with no sheen.

Fixed by the feature documents

  • One line, fixed height; a long path ends in an ellipsis and the time keeps its place. No token count, no second line, no progress or remaining time, no “Still working…”.
  • Shows from the render where the person’s message enters the thread until the turn’s end; gone while a permission card or question waits (7); the activity rows keep their spinner and running head, so two progress marks can show together (5), (3).
  • The provider name goes only on stages without a tool; tool stages use the activity rows’ words (“Reading <path>”, “Running a command”; Codex listFiles and search “Reading files”).
  • The time counts from turn.started in whole seconds and never restarts: 0s · 59s · 1m 0s · 59m 59s · 1h 0m 0s; it stays --text-metadata and never sweeps. Under reduced motion the sweep stops and the words still change.
  • Not a live region, nothing focusable; the time is aria-hidden, and the existing sr-only status announces the stage kind only.

04

Stage copy and elapsed time New, not in code

The wording and elapsed format the person chose (decision 1), written with Doan’s clarify preset: one fact per state, the same words as the shipped activity rows for the same tools (“Reading <path>”, “Running a command”, threadItems.ts runningHead, transport.ts GENERIC_SUMMARY), the app’s “<Provider> is working” for the general stage (decision 11), and no progress Doan does not know. Stages without a tool name the provider; tool stages start with the verb, as the activity rows do, so a long path keeps its room. No “Still working…” text for a long quiet stretch. The time counts from turn.started every second and never restarts with a stage. The visible-text column carries the sweep as the indicator does. The screen-reader column is the existing sr-only status, announced once per stage kind, without paths or time; it is never shown.
