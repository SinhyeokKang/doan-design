Composer list hanging indent — draft

Redesign draft for composer-list-hanging-indent (docs/features/composer-list-hanging-indent/spec.md, design.md): a soft-wrapped row of a composer list item starts at that item’s body start, in all four composers (one shared field). Until this feature’s branch merges, this draft is the /design-sync source for frames 01–07; composer-markdown.html stays as it is (its frame 03 still says the field has no hanging indent).

Copied from shipped code through composer-markdown.html’s restatement: the docked composer box (296) and the Home composer (680) with their tools and tray, the field type (16/24), list markers (bullet drawn over a transparent typed “- ”, numbers as typed, 12 px marker column, marker x 20 + 20 per depth), chips and the skill highlight. New here (not in code): one block per line with the item’s body start as its padding and a negative first-row indent, depth spaces drawn at zero width, the line-start caret before the marker at every depth, and no depth cap. The implementation builds the field on ProseMirror (one paragraph per line, list drawing as decorations, the marker gap as letter-spacing, design.md); the draft draws the same geometry with static blocks and one small script that sets each line’s body start from its marker width.

01

Field · a long item wraps to its body startChanged · not in code

A soft-wrapped row of a list item starts at that item’s body start, at every depth: body x 32 at depth 0, then +20 per depth (52, 72, … 132 at depth 5, with no depth cap, so deep items get a narrow column). Markers stay where they are today (x 20 + 20 per depth). Lines that are not items — plain lines, indented plain lines, a line starting with a chip — still wrap to the field’s left edge. The text is unchanged: markers and depth spaces are typed characters.

02

Field · numbered items align to their own bodyChanged · not in code

Each item’s wrapped rows line up with that item’s own body start, so “9.”, “10.” and “100.” stagger. This differs on purpose from the sent bubble, which aligns a whole list past its widest number: in the field, adding or removing the tenth item while typing must not move the other items’ rows. Numbers show as typed.

03

Field · chips, a long word and an empty itemChanged · not in code

A chip in an item’s body that starts a wrapped row sits at the body start. A word too long for the row breaks anywhere (overflow-wrap: anywhere) and its pieces stay at the body start. An empty item keeps its marker; the caret goes to its body start.

05

Field · at its maximum height, scrolled to the caretChanged · not in code

At the eight-row cap the field scrolls inside its card and keeps the caret in view after Shift+Enter, paste or undo (only the field scrolls). Wrapped rows keep their hanging indent while scrolled.
