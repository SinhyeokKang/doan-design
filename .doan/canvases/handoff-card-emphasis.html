handoff-card-emphasis · draft · 5 frames

A canvas in the thread is one card, never a notice.

Scope: docs/features/handoff-card-emphasis/ (spec ‘확정한 결정’ and ‘완료 조건’, design ‘머리 행’, ‘바탕과 크기’, ‘메타’, ‘CSS 구조’ and ‘드래프트 요청’). A redesign of two shipped cards: the receiving conversation’s handoff card (.doan/canvases/canvas-handoff.html, frame handoff-card) and the turn’s canvas cards (.doan/canvases/canvas-drawing-cards.html); neither old draft is edited. Every card here is new, not in code.

Decided by the person: both cards share one style because both stand for a canvas; a white surface with a 1 px grey border (a grey fill reads as a notice, like “Stopped”); larger than today; richer meta; thumbnails in every window; Open is the only target. Chosen on this board (2026-11-25): a preview banner of the canvas’s top across every card, then the row, whatever the number of cards; until its image arrives, after a failed capture and once the canvas is gone, the banner shows the canvas icon, faint (no spinner: the status line already spins while a card draws). Layouts compared and dropped: a large row without a thumbnail, and a thumbnail row (the name lost its room at 347 and 280). Still open: which meta stays. /design-sync will compare handoff, canvas-cards, stacks and widths; copy is the wording reference.

Widths · 280, the Overview’s 720 and the prototype window New, not in code

A card always fills the thread column. The name is cut in the middle keeping its extension (the shipped cut), the full name in the shipped tooltip; Open never shrinks; the meta wraps by item, never inside one.
