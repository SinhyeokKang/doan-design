A little ground above the conversation

Approved design · all five buddies: Doany, Puri, Dungdung, Popo and Dori · not yet implemented in the app.

A 56px buddy takes short, grounded hops along the input. On canvas pages, after 60–120 s in which the person rests, it hides and reappears at the tool panel. A queued message, suggested prompts or a waiting permission card keeps it away from the input; when they end, it comes back. Anything floating over its spot, such as the picking hint or a tooltip, sends it down until 1 s after it closes.

Click the buddy’s body to send it to the other surface, or to a new spot on the same one when it cannot go there. Its transparent margin lets clicks through.

Use the queue, Hide & reappear, Picking hint, Toolbar tooltip, Fold panel, Suggested prompts, Permission card and Input unavailable controls to review transitions. Other controls replay motion and attention. These are preview controls, not product UI.

The HTML source contains the implementation handoff: confirmed geometry, timing, state priorities, references, review evidence and questions for future feature planning. Frame and element ids are preserved.
