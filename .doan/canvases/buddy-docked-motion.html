A little ground above the conversation

Approved design · Doany + Puri · not yet implemented in the app.

A 56px buddy takes short, grounded hops along the input. On canvas pages, it occasionally hides and reappears at the tool panel. Queue presence keeps it away from the input; clearing the queue brings it back.

Use Add queue, Clear queue and Hide & reappear to review transitions. Other controls replay motion and attention. These are preview controls, not product UI.

The HTML source contains the implementation handoff: confirmed geometry, timing, state priorities, references, review evidence and questions for future feature planning. Frame and element ids are preserved.
