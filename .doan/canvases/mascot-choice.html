mascot-choice — choosing a mascot in first run and Settings

A person chooses one of five mascots in a new first step of first run, and can change it anytime in Settings > General. The mascot shows on Home and the canvas working notice, and sets the accent (ramps: mascot-accent.html). Plan: docs/features/mascot-choice/. Every frame is new and none is in code. /design-sync will compare mascot-step, mascot-step-picked, settings-general, settings-general-failed and notice.

Implementation spec

  1. Mascot step. First run becomes Welcome → Mascot → Agent → Project. The stepper is the shipped capsule with three steps; Get started opens Mascot, Back returns to Welcome, Continue goes to Agent and is always enabled (Doany is chosen by default). Picking a card saves it at once and the accent follows at once. Try the picker under frame 01.
  2. Mascot picker (one component, here and in Settings): a Cover Flow row, a radio group of five doan-panel cards. Each card holds the mascot's card.png at 196 × 196 px, its name and its one line, in a 220 px card (the app copy of card.png is 588 × 588, 3×). The chosen card faces front in the middle with a 2 px ring in the accent icon colour and the Home composer's glow (a new accent place); the others turn 50° toward it at 84 % size, 200 px from the middle and 68 px apart after that, stacked behind it, each with a faint reflection. Picking a card (click, or arrows as in a radio group) moves it to the middle over --time-settle with --ease-enter; under reduced motion it jumps. The row is 1040 px in the Mascot step (its scene is wider than Agent's 560) and is clipped with faded ends in Settings' 48rem column.
  3. Settings > General is a new first category (General, Agents, MCP Connectors, Notifications, About); Settings still opens on Agents the first time. Its one section, Mascot, holds the same cards in a settings group. A failed save shows the shared settings message with Retry, and the cards return to the last saved mascot.
  4. Home shows the chosen mascot in the shipped Doany slot (114 px) with the same motion, from its transparent still.png; not drawn here, only the picture changes. Welcome comes before the choice, so it always shows Doany on violet, whatever is saved.
  5. Working notice: the chosen mascot's painter. Doany keeps picking one of its three variants; a friend has one. Crop boxes: .doan/assets/mascots/README.md. The glow is the mascot's accent.

Copied from shipped code: onboarding, settings page and notice CSS from app/src/renderer/styles.css on develop, checked against the files; the settings shell (header tabs, sidebar) is a schematic, since only its General row changes. Cards and painter GIFs: .doan/assets/mascots/. Copy follows Doan's clarify preset; Doany's one line is new and needs confirmation.
