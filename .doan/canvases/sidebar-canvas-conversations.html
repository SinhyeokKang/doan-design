Conversations under the viewed canvas — draft

Redesign draft for sidebar-canvas-conversations (docs/features/sidebar-canvas-conversations/spec.md, design.md ‘초안 요청’; parent docs/features/canvas-multi-conversation). It builds on canvas-concurrent-conversations (several conversations per canvas, no archive): the sidebar lists the viewed canvas’s conversations with their own status marks. Until this feature merges, this draft is the /design-sync source for its nine frames; shipped drafts stay as they are.

Copied from shipped code: the sidebar column, project row, group head, canvas rows and their menu button rule (ShellSidebar.tsx, the sidebar block of styles.css restated verbatim), the status slot and its marks and labels (SlotMark, conversationList.ts, limitReset.ts, canvasNotice.ts), the chevron pattern of the project row (.shell-lead, .shell-disclosure), the Delete words of the title menu (conversationDelete.ts: “Delete conversation”, deleteItemLabel, the unavailable reasons), and the tooltip. Abbreviated: Search, Home and the resize handle are left out; the column is drawn on the opaque --bg-app fallback.

New here: the conversation rows and their group, the canvas row’s chevron and fold, where the signals sit folded and unfolded, and the trash in the row. Copy: Doan’s clarify preset; presets read: shape, harden, clarify, critique. Icons: lucide-react 1.61.0 paths as installed.

01

Unfolded · the viewed canvas’s conversations New, not in code

Under the canvas row the page shows (aria-current="location"), when it has 2 or more conversations: every one of them, most recent activity first, each with its own status mark (needs your answer, working, queued for the usage limit, finished unread). The conversation the panel shows is the current row (aria-current="true"): a decorative check (the sidebar worktree menu’s current mark) sits in the indent gutter under the canvas icon, so every title keeps its width and the shipped subtle ink; the canvas row keeps its shipped location fill. While the list shows, the canvas row drops its conversation signal; the other canvases keep theirs (orders’ unread bubble counts conversations started anywhere, the panel included). Right: a terminal agent works on checkout, so its spinner stays on the canvas row (“Codex is working in a terminal”) while the list carries the conversations’ own marks. Order freezes while the pointer or focus is in the list; a new conversation joins at the end until both leave.

Folded · the canvas row carries the strongest signal New, not in code

Folded, the canvas row shows its conversations’ strongest signal in the sidebar order: needs your answer > working > queued for the usage limit > finished unread (conversationList.ts RANK). The fold is saved on this device and shared by every canvas: the next canvas you open starts folded too. Right: the only state left is a queued message, so the canvas row shows the clock.

03

Hover · the canvas icon turns into the chevron New, not in code

With the pointer on the viewed canvas row, or keyboard focus in it, its 16px canvas icon gives its place to the chevron, as the project row’s thumbnail does (.shell-lead, .shell-disclosure; the name never moves). The chevron is tabIndex=-1, named “Collapse checkout” / “Expand checkout”, turned −90° while folded; the row’s ← / → fold and unfold it. The row menu button takes the status spot as shipped. Under keyboard focus the row shows the shipped inset ring with the chevron and menu button, and no hover fill. Clicking the row itself while the list is unfolded leaves the panel as it is.

05

Hover · the trash takes the status mark’s place New, not in code

With the pointer on a conversation row (or focus on the row or its trash) the status mark is hidden visually and the trash shows flush at the row’s end, the canvas row menu button’s geometry. It is the title menu’s Delete: tooltip “Delete conversation”, named deleteItemLabel (“Delete “Draft three headline options for the hero””), the shipped alert, main’s check and toast. Middle and right: a working or waiting conversation’s trash stays reachable but unavailable (aria-disabled, disabled ink), and its tooltip and description give the reason.

06

Keyboard · Tab reaches the trash New, not in code

The rows are one tree stop with ↑ / ↓; Tab from a conversation row moves to its trash, as Tab from a canvas row reaches its menu button. → on a conversation row does nothing. With focus on the trash the row shows it in the status mark’s place and the focus ring is on the trash. After a delete from here focus goes to the next row, else the previous one, else the canvas row; Cancel returns it to the trash.

07

180 px · long and empty titles New, not in code

At the sidebar’s minimum width (180 px) conversation titles ellipsize on one line and the full title is the row’s name. An empty title reads “Conversation”. The current row’s check sits in the same gutter (x 34–50) at this width. The status mark and trash keep their 16 / 32 px. The cost of the 58 px indentation (titles under the canvas name): at 180 px a title has about 72 px at rest, and even “Conversation” truncates.
