Scope. Feature projects-home — the projects-home feature documents (retired 2026-10-25; decisions in docs/PRODUCT.md §7, structure in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §7/§13). Home with its project cards and empty state, every card-row state, the add checklist, the folder set-up confirmation, the shared toast, the drop overlay, and the workspace top bar with the worktree switcher and the workspace status line. The remove draft-guard copy and the per-screen menu enable table are tiles inside workspace (docs/PRODUCT.md §7, conductor decision 2026-10-25).

New. home, home-empty, row-states, add-checklist, setup-confirm, toast and drop-overlay are entirely new (the retired projects-home feature documents name the future components Home, ProjectCard, AddProjectsDialog, SetUpFolderConfirm, Toast and DropOverlay; none exist yet, so those frames carry no data-doan-implements). In workspace only the parts with a dashed yellow label are new: the top bar's ← and breadcrumb with the branch chip, the switcher menu, the status line, and the guard copy. The rest of that window copies the shipped shell. Copy that the feature documents do not give was drawn by this draft and is marked “draft copy” in its frame label; the list is in the handoff.

Decisions applied. Feature Q&A 1–4, Q1, R1–R3, R6 (spec.md). Scan behavior (conductor answer 2026-10-25): while scanning, found candidates fill in as “Checking…” (disabled), a progress line shows “n of N”, Add stays disabled until the scan ends, and Cancel stops the remaining checks and closes the dialog without saving anything. Draft decisions (not in the feature documents, for the conductor to accept or change): three-column card grid; a row shows the branch (or path when there is no branch) on line 1 and the folder path on line 2; problem actions sit on their own line under the row; the row and card menus and the worktree switcher are in-app menus (role=menu) so e2e can read them by role and name; a newly added or re-added card gets a 1 px accent outline.

Revision 1 (2026-10-25, conductor review). The workspace right column now copies the shipped Canvas panel (Path, ID, Width, Frames; Comments Open/Resolved; Activity; the hint line) and the shipped Comments section under it.
