buzz-add-projects · draft round 1 · 11 windows and 4 state tiles

Add Projects, as a page in the shell.

Scope: the Add Projects page that replaces the native multi-folder picker, the checklist dialog and the window-wide folder drop (buzz-restyle child 5, docs/features/buzz-add-projects/). Every frame is new and is compared by /design-sync. The page adds one project at a time from a local folder or a GitHub URL, sets a folder up in place, and then goes to Home with that project chosen (2026-10-30 user). The header’s Add Projects and Projects become page tabs; Projects, Cancel and Escape return to the screen the page was opened from. A drop is taken only on the Open a local folder card, here and in first run’s Project step. While a clone runs, nothing leaves the page.

Copied from shipped screens: the header and the sidebar (TopBar in app/src/renderer/App.tsx, app/src/renderer/ShellSidebar.tsx, CSS via .doan/canvases/buzz-search.html, which copies app/src/renderer/styles.css); the white body panel as on Home (.doan/canvases/buzz-home.html, Home.tsx); the whole page body from first run’s Project step (ProjectScreen and projectNotice in app/src/renderer/Onboarding.tsx, its .ob-* rules in app/src/renderer/styles.css at d6f9c0d8, linked ds/ primitives in place); Home’s drop target from the composer (styles.css .drop-target). Canvas-only parts: the page and captions, the wallpaper behind the glass, schematic traffic lights, the tiles. Names and paths are illustrative.

New in this draft (not in code): the page heading and lead (“Add a project”), the Cancel / action footer and the labels “Set up and add” and “Clone and add”, the card drop and its “Drop to add this folder” status, the notices “Drop one folder at a time.” and the new Home composer text, the cloning line “Stay on this page until the clone finishes, or cancel it.”, and the locked header and sidebar while cloning. References: Buzz block/buzz-app at c40dfdf4 (local HEAD ec1b9acc) has no screen for adding a project; its src/app listing was checked on 2026-10-30 and CommunityRepositoriesSettings.tsx is the nearest file, not used. First run’s Project step, itself drawn from Buzz’s onboarding, is the reference. Presets read: shape, clarify, critique.
