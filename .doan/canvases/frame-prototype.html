One frame, one prototype window

Only an explicitly supported frame selected as a frame enables playback. Keep the current canvas marks, toolbar, drawer and black dimensions capsule; add playback beside Export and inside the selected frame’s capsule. The canvas drawer still has one action: move and select. The prototype window has its own header: Back, Forward, Reload and a frame name button that opens a drawer of this canvas’s supported frames.

All four frames are design-sync targets; prototype-window and prototype-states are drawn on the new window shell. Copied from shipped code (checked): the shell and header from styles.css (.workspace.app-shell 8 px inset, .shell-top, .shell-history 32 px chrome icon buttons with the 10 px corner and 4 px gap, the 72 px traffic space) and DESIGN.md ‘App shell’ (header 56 px, Back x124 and Forward x160 at y12 after the sidebar toggle at x88; with no sidebar here, Back takes x88); Back and Forward glyphs are lucide ArrowLeft and ArrowRight as in App.tsx TopBar, Reload is RotateCw as in CanvasHeader.tsx, with the IconTip tooltip its Reload canvas has; the name button is .shell-search (36 px, 10 px inset, pill corner, backdrop fills, subtle 14/20) with the canvas header’s 16 px .chev; the drawer is the canvas frame drawer (ds menu, DESIGN.md ‘Frame drawer list’, CanvasHeader.tsx: sideOffset 4, collisionPadding 8, rows with aria-current="location" and a roving tabindex, the menu’s quiet scrollbar); the state overlay and scene are .problem-overlay, .problem-scene, .problem-icon, .problem-heading, .problem-actions, the reading line .problem-reading, the ghost Details ▸/▾ toggle and .problem-detail from styles.css, ProblemOverlay.tsx, DESIGN.md ‘Problem overlay’ and problem-project.html; focus rings from ds controls.css (2 px, +2) and surfaces.css menu rows (−2). Traffic lights are schematic and --bg-app stands in for the native glass.

Proposals, not copied: the name centred with the chevron after it and the pressed fill while its drawer is open; the drawer centred under the name; “Playing” in the drawer’s Viewing slot and the frames’ canvas numbers; the Reload label “Reload prototype”; the right reserve giving way to the 16 px header inset when the window is narrower than two reserves plus a usable name (the 360 px tiles); the 240 px frame centred in the 360 px minimum window; the state icons (FileWarning, TriangleAlert, SquareDashed, CircleSlash); the reading line on the blur instead of the opaque panel; standard ink for the state body; the still image under failure and lost states; the always-mounted live region; the empty drawer’s line. Same target (Play on the frame already in this window): bring the window forward and keep its input and size. Different frame (a main-window Play or a drawer pick): reuse this window, fit it to that frame within the screen, reset state and add a history entry. Back and Forward play the previous or next entry the same way without adding one, and skip entries whose frame or support mark is gone. Reload restarts the current frame from its initial state (inputs, open modal, scroll and animation), keeps the window size and adds no entry. Main-window navigation or hiding does not change the target; Quit ends both windows.

Content-only scope: inputs, buttons, modals, scrolling and motion. Document navigation, new windows, downloads and native form submit are blocked. Fetch keeps the existing canvas policy. These are required behaviors, not verified prototype capabilities.

Loading, recovery and target loss

Every state reuses the problem overlay (styles.css .problem-overlay): over the content panel only, the panel colour at 60 % with an 8 px blur. Loading shows the shipped reading line (.problem-reading) on that overlay; failures and lost targets show .problem-scene, whose body is standard ink so it holds over dark frames. On a failure or a lost target the previous run is replaced by a still image of its last moment under the blur and stops running; the first load shows an empty panel. With Reduce transparency the overlay is the opaque panel. No loading, failure or lost view counts as a successful prototype render.

Each title names its cause: loading, read failure, initialization failure, frame removal and support removal do not share a title. The title stays a heading (h2, no status role); a polite live region mounted in the window for its whole life announces each state. Back, Forward, Reload and the frame name stay usable above the overlay; Reload on a failure or lost state acts as Retry, and during a load it starts the load again.
