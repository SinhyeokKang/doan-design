buddy-home — Home with each buddy’s accent

Home with a request typed and a preset chosen, once per buddy, to see the chosen buddy and its accent together (ramps: buddy-accent.html; picker: buddy-choice.html; plan: docs/features/buddy-choice/). Only the buddy picture and the accent change; layout, copy and motion are Home as shipped. Send is enabled, so its fill and ink show; hover and pressed are in buddy-accent.html. The suggested prompts hide while the field is filled, as shipped.

Copied from doan-home.html (frame home-filled), with the header and sidebar markup captured from the built app's Home (1238 × 780, .scratch/runtime-test/buddy-home/) and the current Home and shell rules from app/src/renderer/styles.css on develop laid over it (the composer glow replaced the panel-wide atmosphere, and the suggested prompts are rows) and the shipped Doany slot from doany.css. Doany's slot shows the shipped still (public/doany/doany.png). Puri's slot shows its transparent still.png. The remaining friends show their cards with mix-blend-mode: multiply, so their soft shadows show; the app uses the still with Doany's motion. Not in code: a ground shadow layer under the buddy (shadow.png, the contact shadow lifted from its card), behind the still at the same coordinates and padding; it stays on the ground and shrinks and fades during a hop (see buddy-assets). Puri has it; Doany's comes next, and each friend's arrives with its layers.
