Lists in the composer and the sent bubble

Scope: docs/features/composer-markdown/ (spec ‘완료 조건’ 1–12, design ‘입력창 목록 그리기’ and ‘초안 요청’). The field draws lines that start with - , * or a 1–3 digit N. as list items; the sent bubble draws them as nested lists with a hanging indent; new conversation titles and queue rows drop the markers. Nothing else in Markdown is drawn: **, backticks, # and --- stay plain text. The text the field holds, sends and copies is unchanged (grey boxes under the tiles show it, spaces included).

/design-sync compares all seven frames, except board annotations (captions, tile labels, the grey text boxes and the key notes). Elements to pair carry data-doan-id; the new spans are .list-mark (with data-kind="ul" or "ol") and .list-depth (data-depth), the bubble blocks .bubble-list, .bubble-item, .bubble-item-body and .bubble-text.

Copied from shipped code, not redesigned (develop fc50b992f, read in this session): the docked composer (box and capsule), its tray and queue rows (DockedComposer.tsx, ComposerParts.tsx, docs/design/canvas-conversation.md), the Home composer (HomeComposerView.tsx, docs/design/home.md), the panel head, thread, bubble, anchor chip and skill word (ConversationThread.tsx, CanvasConversationPanel.tsx), and the sidebar canvas and conversation rows (ShellSidebar.tsx); rules restated from app/src/renderer/styles.css, markup from .doan/canvases/provider-commands.html, agent-reply-chips.html and sidebar-canvas-conversations.html. Field states are drawn at the docked composer’s 296 px (panel 320, the default at 960 × 600) and Home’s 680 px.

References: the Claude desktop app composer, from the user’s screenshots (spec ‘범위 근거’, 2026-11-19 conversation): - drawn as •, ordered numbers shown as typed, one indent step per depth, **bold** and --- left as text. Not checked by me in that app. Depth by the parent item’s body column follows CommonMark’s list item rule, recalled, not re-read. Icons: lucide paths as used in the canvases above.

Example data is illustrative: project acme-web, canvas landing (frame hero “Hero”, element cta button “Get started”), a project skill copy-check.

Settled in this draft

  1. Indent and marker column, in the field and the bubble: every marker stands one --space-5 step (20 px) in and each depth adds one more step; the marker column is --space-3 (12 px), so a bullet is followed by a short gap and a number by its typed space. In the bubble a list’s marker column grows to its widest marker, so the bodies of one list line up; in the field a wider marker (100. ) pushes only its own item’s text (frames 02 and 05, tiles c).
  2. Markers take the body ink (--text-standard): the • and the typed numbers. In the field each marker starts at its step; in the bubble the • starts the column and numbers are right-aligned in it, so 9. and 10. keep the same gap to their text.
  3. No extra spacing between list items in the bubble or the field: lines keep the spacing they had as plain text.

The person’s list differs from the agent’s Markdown list on purpose: the agent’s replies keep 4 px between items and a circle at the second bullet level (.md lists in styles.css); the person’s bubble keeps the lines as typed and draws • at every level.

Keys

Shift+Enter, Tab, Shift+Tab, Ctrl+Tab and Backspace cannot be drawn; the notes beside field-bullets and field-ordered give each rule, and tiles b show the field right after a Shift+Enter. The rules are the same in all four composers (Home, the Overview’s docked composer, the canvas panel and the prototype window), which share one field.

Keys in a list item

Enter
Sends, as today.
Shift+Enter
New line with the same depth and marker (- after - , * after * ). On an empty item: one level out at depth 1 or more; at depth 0 the marker goes and the list ends.
Tab
Moves the item one level in. If there is no item above to nest under, focus moves as today.
Shift+Tab
Moves the item one level out. At depth 0, focus moves as today.
Ctrl+Tab
Always moves focus, with or without Shift, inside a list too.
Backspace
At the start of an item’s text with nothing selected: one level out at depth 1 or more; at depth 0 the whole marker goes and the line becomes plain text.
← →
Never stop inside the hidden - or * .

Tab and Shift+Tab act only when the field does not already use Tab: the next-message suggestion in an empty field and the open / list keep it. Each list edit is one undo step. While a Korean or other IME is composing, the field is not redrawn, and Shift+Enter or Tab waits until the composition ends. Copy gives plain text with the markers and depth spaces, never a •. On a line that is not a list item, every key works as today.

Keys in a numbered item

Shift+Enter
Adds the next number at the same depth. If that number would have four digits (after 999. ), it is an ordinary line break.
Tab
Moves the item under the one above and renumbers it 1. , the first item of a nested numbered list.
Shift+Tab, Backspace
As in a bullet item (frame 01). Backspace at depth 0 removes the whole 12. .

A depth is the parent item’s text column in spaces (3 under 1. , 2 under - ; a tab counts as 4). Drawn, every depth is one step.

05

Sent bubble · nested listsNew · not in code

The person’s bubble in the canvas panel’s thread draws list lines as nested lists: one step per depth and a hanging indent, so a wrapped line starts under the item’s text. Numbers are shown as typed, so two items typed 1. both read 1.. Anchor chips and skill words stay where they were typed inside an item. A line that starts with a chip is not a list item, and neither is a line that starts with a year. Selecting the bubble and pressing ⌘C copies the sent text exactly, markers and depth spaces included; no Copy button.

The list’s marker column takes the width of 100. , its widest marker, so all three bodies start at the same x and wrapped lines hang under them. Another list in the same bubble, or a nested one, sizes its own column.

What the bubble draws

Items
Lines that start with - , * or a 1–3 digit N. , and their depth by the parent item’s text column.
Numbers
As typed, never renumbered: two items typed 1. both read 1., as in tile b.
Wrapping
Under the item’s text (hanging indent), in the bubble only.
Columns
One marker column per list, as wide as its widest marker (at least 12 px), so 9., 10. and 100. items start their text together.
Not items
A line that starts with a chip, a 4-digit number or anything else; it keeps its spaces as plain text.
Copy
⌘C on a selection copies the sent text, markers and depth spaces included.

07

Titles and queue rows drop list markersNew · not in code

A conversation started by a message that begins with a list is titled with its text only: the markers and depth spaces at line starts go, and lines join with one space, as today. The same rule reads a queued message in its row. Titles saved before this change keep their markers; nothing rewrites them. Edit and resend, a restored draft and a queued message taken back into the field bring back the text as sent, markers included.
