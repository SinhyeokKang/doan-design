buddy-choice — choosing a buddy in first run and Settings

A person chooses one of five buddies in a new first step of first run, and can change it anytime in Settings > General. The buddy shows on Home and the canvas working notice, and sets the accent (ramps: buddy-accent.html). Plan: docs/features/buddy-choice/. Every frame is new and none is in code. /design-sync will compare buddy-step, buddy-step-picked, buddy-step-end, buddy-step-failed, settings-general, settings-general-failed and notice.

Implementation spec

  1. Buddy step. First run becomes Welcome → Buddy → Agent → Project. The stepper is the shipped capsule with three steps; Get started opens Buddy, Back returns to Welcome, Continue goes to Agent and is always enabled (Doany is chosen by default). Picking a card saves it at once and the accent follows at once. Try the picker under frame 01.
  2. Buddy picker (one component, here and in Settings): a Cover Flow row, a radio group of five doan-panel cards. Each card holds the buddy's card.png at 196 × 196 px, its name (label-sm at semibold) and its one line (caption in standard ink), in a 244 px card with 20 px top and bottom and 24 px side padding (the app copy of card.png is 588 × 588, 3×). The chosen card faces front in the middle with a 2 px ring in the accent icon colour and the Home composer's glow (a new accent place); the others turn 50° toward it at 84 % size, min(200 px, 21 %), min(268 px, 28 %), min(316 px, 32 %) and min(364 px, 37 %) of the row from the middle, stacked behind it. No reflection. The row is linear in its own order, Popo · Saemi · Doany · Dungdung · Dori, so the default Doany sits in the middle with two buddies on each side and all five show from the first visit; the ends are ends. Three inputs move it, all through one select that checks the card, saves it and moves the row: clicking a side card, ← / → while the group has focus (as in a radio group), and a horizontal scroll (a two-finger trackpad swipe, or Shift + mouse wheel), one card per gesture (horizontal wheel delta past a threshold, then ignored until the gesture ends, so one swipe never skips several). Arrows and swipes stop at Popo and Dori (the arrows override the native radio wrap). No prev/next buttons: the side cards are the controls, and a side card's shadow deepens on hover. The move takes --time-settle with --ease-enter; under reduced motion it jumps. The row is up to 1040 px in the Buddy step (its scene is wider than Agent's 560; the heading, notice and footer keep the other steps' 512 px column, so Back and Continue sit where they do on Agent and Project) and fits a 960 × 600 window. Each card's radio is named by the buddy's name and described by its line. A failed save shows first run's panel notice (as the Agent step's list notice, doan-notice doan-panel) under the row, naming the pick and the buddy that stays, with Retry; Continue goes on with the saved buddy.
  3. Settings > General is static: one shared preference row per buddy, stacked in a settings group, each with its card.png at 48 × 48 px (--size-control-lg) 12 px before the text, its name and its one line in the shared preference row type (as Notifications), and first run's radio at the end. Nothing moves on a pick and there is no accent ring; the radio shows the choice.
  4. Settings > General is a new first category (General, Agents, MCP Connectors, Notifications, About); Settings still opens on Agents the first time. Its one section, Buddy, lists the five buddies as rows in a settings group. A failed save shows a notice above the group (where shared settings notices sit), naming the pick and the buddy that stays, with Retry; the radio returns to the saved buddy.
  5. Home shows the chosen buddy in the shipped Doany slot (114 px) with the same motion, from its transparent still.png; not drawn here, only the picture changes. Welcome comes before the choice, so it always shows Doany on violet, whatever is saved.
  6. Working notice: the chosen buddy's painter. Doany keeps picking one of its three variants; a friend has one. Crop boxes: .doan/assets/buddies/README.md. The glow is the buddy's accent.

Copied from shipped code: onboarding, settings page and notice CSS from app/src/renderer/styles.css on develop, checked against the files; the Settings header and sidebar are the built app's markup (captured at 1238 × 780), with the General row added first and MCP Connectors shown as for a signed-in Claude Code or Codex. Cards and painter GIFs: .doan/assets/buddies/. Copy follows Doan's clarify preset.
