Decision
Report §6 U2: the docked composers (Overview, canvas panel, prototype window) ask “Replace your draft?” as Home does before Edit and resend replaces another draft; a queue returned after a failed or stopped turn is protected the same way. Record it in PRODUCT item 3.
Evidence
As is: ConversationThread.tsx:885 (Edit and resend) → conversationStore.ts:290-302 editAndResend, which refuses only when the draft changed while files were issued again, then dropHeld and overwrites the draft. Home: homeConversations.ts:86 restoreRequest and the dialog at HomeComposerView.tsx:594-597. Returned queue: conversationStore.ts:364-395 takeDraft; PRODUCT 249, 260.
What changes
Reuse Home's dialog as is: dialog.doan-dialog[data-ui-size="compact"], title “Replace your draft?”, Cancel “Keep Draft” (subtle, first, autofocus) and “Replace” (prominent, last), centred over the window with the scrim. Ask under restoreRequest's rule: only when the draft is not empty and differs from the last request (text, preset, attachments); an empty or identical draft is replaced at once, as today. The body is proposed: Home says “…the last request you sent to <project>”, but a docked composer belongs to one conversation of the project, so it names the conversation. Keep the strings shared with Home: B4 (D4) renames “Keep Draft” to “Keep draft”, and both dialogs should change together.
Open detail, chosen
Esc behaves as Keep Draft and returns focus to the field (U4, in B2, fixes Home's Esc; build the docked one that way from the start). The dialog takes aria-label as Home's does; whether it also takes role="alertdialog" and aria-describedby is the conductor's choice (B2 leaves it open for Home too).
States to test
  1. Fail a turn with two messages queued behind it (canvas panel). The queue comes back as the draft. Press Edit and resend: the dialog opens with focus on Keep Draft.
  2. Keep Draft (or Esc): the dialog closes, the draft and its held attachments stay, focus is in the field at the end of the text.
  3. Replace: the draft becomes the last request (state As is 2), held attachments of the old draft are released.
  4. Empty field, or a draft equal to the last request: Edit and resend replaces at once, no dialog.
  5. Repeat in the Overview and in the prototype window's panel.
