canvas-chip · draft · 6 frames

Put another canvas into a message as a chip.

Scope: docs/features/canvas-chip/ (spec ‘확정한 결정’ and ‘완료 조건’, design ‘끌기’, ‘메뉴와 놓기의 판정’, ‘누르기’ and ‘드래프트 요청’). A canvas row dragged from the sidebar, or its menu’s Add to message, puts a chip for that whole canvas into the composer of the canvas panel, the Overview or Home, when the chip’s root is the composer’s root. The agent gets the canvas’s repository-relative path and id. Every canvas chip, the drag image, the drop place, the refusing composer, the menu item and every new sentence here are new, not in code.

Frames /design-sync compares: chip, drag, menu, sent, home. copy is the wording table. Where the feature leaves a shape to the draft, the frame draws the candidates side by side, labelled, with one recommended; the person picks in the app.

  • The composer card, field, tools and tray (Home’s and the docked one at the canvas panel’s 347 and the Overview’s 720), the anchor chip and its sent form inside a bubble, the .chip-note and .chip-missing lines and the composer reason line: app/src/renderer/styles.css, ComposerParts.tsx, DockedComposer.tsx, HomeComposerView.tsx, chipField.ts and ConversationThread.tsx (SentChip), read for this draft; docs/design/conversation.md and home.md.
  • The sidebar’s project, group and canvas rows, the canvas row menu (.shell-canvas-menu, compact floating, opaque popover fill) and its unavailable item with the reason under the label (shell-menu-reasoned): ShellSidebar.tsx canvasRowMenu, docs/design/shell.md ‘Sidebar groups’. The canvas icons are the sidebar’s: PanelsTopLeft for .doan/canvases/, SwatchBook for .doan/ds/.

The reply-chip refusal sentences (App.tsx replyChipRefusal), the 32-place limit (canvasPicker.ts ANCHOR_LIMIT_TEXT), the project reading and problem lines (problemView.ts) and “Can’t read this canvas.” (canvasRowMenu.ts) are reused word for word.

Canvas chip in the composer New, not in code

A canvas chip names the canvas by its file-name stem and carries the sidebar row’s icon. It edits as one unit, counts once however many times it appears, and shares the 32-place limit with frame, element and point chips. Its tooltip is the repository-relative path; a screen reader hears “Canvas” before the name.

First item of the shipped menu, so the keyboard can do what the drag does. It puts the chip at the caret of the composer on the current screen (the canvas panel, unfolding it if folded; the Overview; Home) and moves focus there. When it cannot, it stays in the arrow order, unavailable, with the reason under its label as the shipped Export… and Move to Trash do; the reason is also its description. A row that cannot be added cannot be dragged either.

Home · chips follow the chosen project and worktree New, not in code

Home’s composer takes chips for the project and worktree chosen above it. The last request, the start draft, a returned start, Edit on Home and a draft restored after quitting keep them. Switching the project or worktree turns chips for the old root into their names in plain text, once, and says so in the composer’s reason line; switching back does not bring them back.
