Keep out of Git in a folder that isn’t a Git repository: Doan creates the repository, after the person agrees.

Scope: docs/features/keep-out-git-init/ (spec ‘완료 조건’ 1–9, design ‘화면’ and ‘초안 요청’). The screens are shipped; what is new on each is named in its caption and tile titles: the .git/ line in the Set Up form, the line under the Doan files card in the setup panel, Project Settings’ enabled Keep out of Git with its new reason, its confirmation and its result line, and the copy of three errors. /design-sync compares setup-form, setup-panel and project-settings; copy is the wording and accessibility reference.

Copied from shipped screens, checked in code on develop: ProjectSetupDialog.tsx and StorageChoice.tsx (Set Up form, Will add, CHIPS), Onboarding.tsx and projectStep.ts (setup panel, StorageCard, the notice slot and its actions), ProjectSettingsPage.tsx, storageRules.ts and SettingsDialog.tsx (Doan files section, Saving and Saved, write error, the Share confirmation), with the CSS restated from styles.css as local-doan-files.html restates it and docs/DESIGN.md ‘Where Doan files are kept’, ‘Notices’ and ‘Shared dialog sizes’. References: none taken from other tools. Sample paths are illustrative. Choices the feature documents left open were answered by the person and are marked “confirmed”.
