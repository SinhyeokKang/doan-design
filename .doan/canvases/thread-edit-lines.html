thread-edit-lines · confirmed 2026-11-25 · 4 frames

Each edit in the thread says how many lines it added and removed.

Scope: docs/features/thread-edit-lines/ (spec ‘확정한 결정’ and ‘완료 조건’ 2–6, design ‘UI’ and ‘드래프트 요청’, tasks T4). Only the line counts are new, not in code: the activity row, its folded head, its steps and the running head are shipped. /design-sync compares steps, heads and widths; copy is the wording reference. The prototype window's conversation panel follows the canvas panel's rules and is not drawn separately.

Fixed by the feature documents

  • Every edit gets the same rule, canvas HTML in .doan/canvases/ included. Line counts only: no diff body, no file open, no revert.
  • A step shows +N −M after its path. A changed line counts as one added and one removed. Several edits of one file are counted one by one.
  • Both sides always show, zero included (+48 −0, +0 −0), so a known zero differs from an unknown count, which shows no numbers at all.
  • The folded head adds the total of the steps whose counts are known (6 steps · +42 −10), also when some edits have none. A row with no known counts keeps today's head. While the head spins with the indicator's words it shows no total.
  • Counts arrive after their step (a separate event) and are never announced; the line stays one line and the thread's height does not change.
