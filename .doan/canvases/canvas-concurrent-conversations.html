Concurrent conversations on one canvas — draft

Redesign draft for canvas-concurrent-conversations (docs/features/canvas-concurrent-conversations/spec.md, design.md ‘초안 요청’; parent docs/features/canvas-multi-conversation): a canvas has no current conversation, nothing is archived, several conversations can work at once. Until this feature merges, this draft is the /design-sync source for its seven frames; shipped drafts stay as they are.

Copied from shipped code: the notice capsule, artist, glow and Stop (CanvasAgent.tsx); the panel head with title menu, New conversation (SquarePen) and Hide (CanvasConversationPanel.tsx); row marks, labels and times (ConversationLine.tsx StatusMark, overviewConversation.ts); the menu’s error line (.conv-error); thread notices and the docked composer’s capsule; the Show conversation badges (CanvasHeader.tsx); Overview rows with their Delete (CanvasConversationList.tsx); the prototype window’s list-failure alert. Styles are the shipped single-line rules of styles.css, plus canvas-handoff.html’s thread restatement. Abbreviated, not redesigned: the zoom control in the canvas header (a “100%” trigger), the composer tray, the thread content, the Overview thumbnails (mock fills).

New here: following a conversation other than the shown one, “Stop ” + quoted title and its short hold, the working badge in the main window, the Overview row head (conversation title) with the canvas caption line, the prototype head menu, the promotion notice’s words, and where the panel lands after a delete. Copy: Doan’s clarify preset; presets read: shape, harden, clarify, critique. Icons: lucide-react 1.61.0 paths as installed, checked.
