mcp-connectors · draft round 1 · 11 frames

MCP Connectors: let Claude Code and Codex use remote MCP servers in Doan conversations.

Scope: docs/features/mcp-connectors/ (spec completion conditions 1–10, design ‘초안 요청’). A new Settings category, MCP Connectors, between Agents and Notifications: add a preset (Notion, Atlassian, Figma) or a https:// URL, connect each agent with its own official MCP sign-in, disconnect per agent, remove a connector. Plus three additions to shipped surfaces: a failure toast, one sentence in Agents’ sign-out confirmation, and two items in a Codex conversation. Everything on this canvas that belongs to MCP Connectors is new and not in code: the category and its sidebar row (Plug icon), its lead, the connector sections, every connector state line and its notices, the Add connector menu, the Custom URL dialog, the Remove confirmation and all their copy. Copy and connector data are real English UI text; account data, conversation text and the long URL connector are illustrative.

/design-sync compares frames 1–8 (empty, list, add-menu, custom-url, connecting, failed, remove-alert, running-notice), the windows and the tiles inside them. Frames 9–11 (file-error, elsewhere, conversation) add to shipped surfaces and follow their measured values; they are not compared.

Copied from shipped screens, not redesigned: the Settings shell (header with sidebar toggle, Back, Forward and the page tabs; the Settings sidebar; Home’s white panel; the 48rem column, title, lead, sections, groups and rows), from app/src/renderer/SettingsPage.tsx, ShellSidebar.tsx, App.tsx TopBar and styles.css, checked against docs/DESIGN.md ‘Settings’ and zero-setup-agents.html (agents frame). Agent rows, waiting and error notices, Retry and the stop confirmation follow Settings › Agents (SettingsAgents.tsx, agentAuthView.ts, Onboarding.tsx NoticeView, StopDialog, PasteDialog). The toast follows Toast.tsx; the conversation panel and thread follow styles.css (buzz-canvas-conversation) and ConversationThread.tsx; Codex’s permission options and their order (Allow, Deny, Allow for this session) come from main/conversation/transport.ts. Shared primitives come from the linked tokens.css and ds/index.css. Not copied: the sidebar row height and colours were not re-measured here; the shell CSS was read from source, not measured.

References: docs/DESIGN.md ‘Adopted rules’, ‘Doan adjustments’ (no coloured edge stripes; errors with an icon), ‘Notices’, ‘Settings’, ‘Canvas conversation panel’ (checked in the repo); docs/PRODUCT.md §5 item 0 (Settings) (checked). Lucide icon paths (Plug, PanelLeftClose, PanelRightClose, SquarePen, Terminal, CircleStop) were read from the installed lucide-react 1.61.0 (app/node_modules); the others are copied from zero-setup-agents.html. Design presets read: shape, clarify, harden (by the lead), critique.
