Revision recheck — draft

Scope. What a human sees when they come back after handing comments to an agent: who resolved what and when, a notice when resolved comments are waiting, a fixed Review bar that steps through them, the Done summary, success status lines after Resolve and Reopen, editing a resolved comment, a missing target, Activity kinds and the bottom label, and the Home and Overview counts. Source: docs/features/revision-recheck/spec.md and design.md (draft request at the end of design.md).

New frames (all new surfaces, not in code): review-notice, review-bar, review-done. /design-sync compares these three. The other frames show shipped screens with the feature’s changes drawn on them; every changed or added part is marked with a dashed “new” tag.

Copied from shipped screens. Comment cards, editor, messages and dialog: app/src/renderer/CommentsPanel.tsx and the comments rules of styles.css (DESIGN.md “Comments — initial comments-ui implementation”). Canvas panel and Activity: RightPanel.tsx, activity.ts. Bottom label: App.tsx .updated. Home rows: Home.tsx, homeCards.ts (DESIGN.md “Projects home”). Overview cards: ProjectOverview.tsx (DESIGN.md “Design navigation”). Go menu items: app/src/main/index.ts. Jump highlight: .mark.jump-highlight (design-nav).

Drawn on top of comments-readability. The chain ships comments-readability (link 2) before this feature (link 3), so every shipped panel here shows its result, copied from its confirmed draft doan/comments-readability.html (commit 8989ac4 on feat-review-loop-drafts) and docs/features/comments-readability/design.md §1–§5: one-line targetLabel target lines (accent button when locatable, --text-2 text when not), “Not found in the current canvas.” beside Location unavailable, the sticky “Comments” title above the head, head buttons “Comment on canvas” / “Comment on selection”, no “Reopen this comment before copying.” on resolved cards, Canvas info without COMMENTS and with the hint above Activity, and the panel-coloured background behind the bottom label. “Copy all open” and the card “Copy” stay; agent-handoff comes after this feature. Activity “Moved · from …” follows revision-continuity. The conductor aligns the shared visuals (banner order, Home summary line) across drafts.

References. No outside product was consulted; every pattern here comes from the shipped app, the existing drafts in doan/, and the sibling chain drafts doan/comments-readability.html and doan/project-feedback.html (all checked, not recalled). Proposed copy where the documents give none is listed in the frames and in the handoff.

Not visible — keyboard, focus, names

Moving to another step puts focus on Next (or Done). Closing Review (× or Esc) returns focus to the notice’s Review button, or to the Comments heading when the notice is gone. Esc handles, in order: an open dialog → the editor → Review (close) → selection (clear). ⌘] / ⌘[ follow Next / Previous; they do not fire inside text inputs. The bar is a region named “Review”; × is named “Close Review”.

Last-review time: Next raises it to the resolve time of the step just passed; Esc or × keep that progress; Done raises it to the end of the set fixed at start. Moving to another canvas or Home ends Review and keeps passed progress. Reload or a moved canvas keeps the same step by comment id. Edit and reopen… opens the Save and reopen editor (frame edit-reopen).

The same focus rule applies when an agent’s resolve removes the focused card. “Show” (after Save and reopen) switches the status filter to Open and locates that comment.

Activity and bottom label

new: Changed, Moved, Resolved kindschanged: bottom label “Updated” → latest kind, HH:MM

Activity (newest first, shipped HH:MM:SS) separates opening, a reload where the canvas row rev changed, a reload where it did not, a move, and comments that turned resolved on a re-read (resolver names, including a human’s own Resolve). The first read on open is the baseline and is not recorded. The bottom label shows the latest of Opened / Changed / Reloaded / Moved as “Kind HH:MM”; a Resolved entry does not change it. A date is added when the time is not today. This replaces the 2026-10-22 app-ui “Updated” definition.
